## 2. Quartz types, what each does, Normal vs Rare vs SR

Quartz = crystal circuit slotted into an orbment. It grants (a) an Art if it is an Art quartz, (b) stats, (c) a passive/effect. Element is one of 7: Earth, Water, Fire, Wind, Time, Space, Mirage. That element also counts toward the orbment line / set effect.

### (a) Art quartz, 4 tiers per element in CS3, 5 in CS1/CS2
Each base Art quartz exists as Normal, R (Rare), SR (Super Rare in CS3; (R) only in CS1/CS2). R/SR add stats on top of the same Art.
- CS3 Earth example: Needle Shoot (base, 50 Earth sepith) to Needle Shoot R (STR+4/DEF+8) to Needle Shoot SR (STR+8/DEF+16). Same pattern for Ivy Nail, Megalith Fall, Gravion Hammer, Crest, La Crest, Earth Pulse, Adamantine Shield.
- CS1 example: Needle Shoot to Needle Shoot (R) STR+2/DEF+3; Earth Lance (R) STR+3/DEF+5; Grand Press (R) STR+5/DEF+7; Yggdrasil (R) STR+7/DEF+10; Ancient Glyph (R) STR+10/DEF+15. CS1 has no SR tier for Art quartz, R only. CS3 adds SR. CS2: UNVERIFIED whether SR exists, Neoseeker CS2 table shows R only in the lines I saw; do not assume SR in CS2.
- CS3 Water: Aqua Bleed, Crystal Edge, Blue Ascension, Diamond Nova; plus Tear/Teara/Tearal, Thelas/Athelas. Fire: Fire Bolt, Venom Flame, Flare Butterfly, Zeruel Cannon; plus Forte/La Forte, Heat Up, Impassion. Wind: Air Strike, Nemesis Arrow, Aerial Dust, Ixion Volt; plus Breath/Holy Breath, Recuria. Time: Soul Blur, Calvary Edge, Lost Genesis; plus Chrono Drive/Break/Burst. Space: Golden Sphere, Cross Crusade, Seventh Caliber; plus Fortuna/Shining, Seraphic Ring. Mirage: Silver Thorn, Galion Fort, Albion Wolf; plus Analyze, Saintly Force, Crescent Mirror.

### (b) Stat quartz, numbered 1-2-3, no Art
Pure stat boosts, upgradeable in tiers. CS3 examples, verified:
- Earth: Defense 1 DEF+20, Defense 2 DEF+40, Defense 3 DEF+80; Break 1 STR+5/Break +20%, Break 2 STR+10/+40%, Break 3 STR+20/+60%
- Water: HP 1 HP+500, HP 2 +1000, HP 3 (UNVERIFIED value, not in lines read, do not quote); Shield 1 ADF+10, Shield 2 +20, Shield 3 (same flag)
- Fire: Attack 1 STR+10, Attack 2 +20, Attack 3 +40; Strike 1 Crit +3%, Strike 2 +6%, Strike 3 +9%
- Wind: Evade 1 EVA+5%, Evade 2 +10%; Move 1 MOV+5, Move 2 +10% (CS1/CS2: Move 3 exists, UNVERIFIED in CS3)
- Time: Action 1 SPD+3, Action 2 +6, Action 3 (UNVERIFIED in CS3 lines read)
- Space: Hit 1 ACC+50%, Hit 2 +100%; EP Cut 1 Arts EP -10%, EP Cut 2 -15%, EP Cut 3 (UNVERIFIED); EP 1 EP+50, EP 2 +100
- Mirage: Mind 1 ATS+?, Mind 2/3 (values UNVERIFIED, not in lines read); Cast 1/2/3 (cast time reduction, values UNVERIFIED)
- GameFAQs CS3 abridged summary, verified as a summary: Earth = DEF/Break, Water = HP/ADF/Magic EVA, Fire = STR/Crit, Wind = MOV/EVA, Time = SPD/Cast Speed, Space = EP Cut/ACC, Mirage = EP/ATS.

### (c) Ailment / on-hit quartz, no Art, adds chance to attacks/crafts
CS3: Poison / Poison II / Poison III (15%/20%/25%), Petrify I/II/III, Seal, Burn, Mute, Freeze, Blind, Sleep, Faint, plus stat-down-on-hit: Shield Breaker (DEF down), Sword Breaker (STR down), Leg Breaker / Speed Breaker (SPD/MOV down, seen in CS3 trade lists), Impede (cancel Arts, Delay+3). CS1 versions are single-tier (Poison 10%, Petrify 10%) with different values, do not carry CS3 percentages back to CS1.

### (d) Bell quartz, R and SR, element-specific, halve Art delay
- Earth Bell (R): ATS+15, delay halved for Earth Arts. Steel Bell (SR): grants Megalith Fall, ATS+30, Earth Arts damage +30%, delay halved.
- Fire Bell / Pyre Bell (grants Flare Butterfly, ATS+30, Fire damage +30%, delay halved), Water Bell / Ocean Bell (same pattern, UNVERIFIED exact Art granted, do not quote), Wind Bell / Aeolus Bell, Time Bell / Black Bell, Space Bell / ?, Mirage Bell / ?, only Earth and Fire Bell SR Arts were verified in the lines read. Others are pattern-inferred, flag as UNVERIFIED.
- Obtained via Trade Shops / boss drops / chests, not plain synthesis in CS3.

### (e) Special R/SR quartz, not plain Art quartz, often multi-Art or unique passive, Trade Shop / drop / chest only in CS3
Verified CS3 Earth: Septium Vein (EP+40/ATS+5, more sepith drops), Dragon Vein (EP+80/ATS+10, Brave/Assault points gradually recover on field), Impenetrable Shield (SR: grants La Crest + Earth Pulse + Adamantine Shield, HP+1200/DEF+48/ADF+48/ATS+24), Genbu (SR: STR+40, Poison/Petrify 30% on hit), Rakan Gem (SR: STR+20/DEF+20, Break +80%, more sepith), Acala Gem (SR: grants Gravion Hammer, HP+1000/DEF+100, Brave points recover on field).

### Synthesis, how Quartz are made
- Where: Orbal Factories / Orbal Workshops / Charging Stations in CS3; Orbal Factories in CS1/CS2, plus Trade Shops (Micht's Pawn Shop, Emmrod's Used Goods, Jackass' Repair Shop in CS1; Trade Shops in CS3) for R/SR and special quartz.
- Cost, base Art quartz: sepith of that element, e.g. CS3 Needle Shoot 50 Earth sepith, Ivy Nail 100, Megalith Fall 200, Gravion Hammer 400. CS1 costs differ and sometimes need a second element: Earth Lance 80 Earth + 20 Fire, Grand Press 200 Earth + 50 Space, Earth Pulse 120 Earth + 30 Water, Hydro Cannon 200 Water + 50 Mirage, Athelas 400 Water + 100 Space.
- Cost, R upgrade (CS3): 3x base quartz + U-Material x2. SR upgrade (CS3): 3x R quartz + U-Material x8. CS1 R: 2x base + U-Material x1 (x2-3 for top tiers). CS2 R: 1x base + U-Material x2/4/6/8 in the Neoseeker lines seen, CS2 upgrade formula is UNVERIFIED as a general rule, those numbers are per-item in that table.
- Stat quartz tier 2/3: sepith OR 3x lower tier + U-Material x1 (tier 2) / x4 (tier 3) in CS3; CS1 similar with sepith amounts 100/250/600 etc.
- Special quartz: some are not synthesizable (CS1 Harvest, Genbu, Impenetrable Shield, Acala Gem), chest / quest reward / shop only. In CS3 many special quartz are Trade Shop recipes requiring 1-3 named quartz + U-Material x6-16, e.g. Impenetrable Shield SR = La Crest SR + Earth Pulse SR + Adamantine Shield SR + U-Material x12.
- Sepith: elemental shards dropped by enemies, also "All Sepith x50/100/200" from chests. Sepith can be traded for mira at shops (CS3: 8 mira per sepith for most elements, 10 for one, 20 for Mirage, the shop table columns were not labelled in the snippet, flag exact per-element mira rates as UNVERIFIED). Sepith is the synthesis currency, not a separate item in the bag in the same way.

## 3. Master Quartz / Core Quartz equivalents

- CS1/CS2 (ARCUS): 1 Master Quartz slot per character. It levels up by battle EXP, max level 5 in CS1/CS2 (UNVERIFIED for CS1, CS2 table shows levels 1-5; CS3 is 7, see below). It grants: large stat boosts (HP/EP/STR/DEF/ATS/ADF/SPD, growing per level), a list of Arts of its element learned at set levels, and 1-3 special abilities.
  - CS2 count, verified from Kiseki Wiki header: 31 Master Quartz in CS2. Examples verified: Iron (Earth, Machias default; Lv1 Needle Shoot + Crest, HP 1500/EP 90/STR 80/DEF 120; ability: higher remaining HP = less damage taken), Megalith (Earth), Aegis (Earth, Millium default; Lv2 Needle Shoot/Earth Pulse/Earth Lance), Tauros (Earth), Canon (Water; Lv1 Tear + Aqua Bleed), Scepter (Water), Force (Fire; Lv1 Fire Bolt), Rebellion (Fire; Lv4 Luminous Ray/Saintly Force/Silver Thorn/Phantom Phobia/Crescent Mirror, i.e. a Master Quartz can grant Arts outside its own element in CS2, flag: CS3 changes this).
- CS3 (ARCUS II): 2 Master Quartz slots, Main and Sub. Verified by Gematsu + RPGFan + GameFAQs.
  - Main: 100% EXP, full stats, all special abilities, Arts list of its element.
  - Sub: 50% EXP, first ability only, Arts list of its element, reduced stats (HP/EP halved, STR 4/5 in the GameFAQs table, other stat reductions UNVERIFIED).
  - Max level 7 in CS3.
  - CS3 count, verified from GameFAQs: 28 Master Quartz, 4 per element, 1,512 possible Main+Sub combos (excluding 2 unique EX Master Quartz: Olivier's Regalia, Aurelia's Valhalla).
  - CS3 rule change, verified: Master Quartz in CS3 only contain Arts from their own element (unlike CS2 Rebellion above).
  - Names seen in CS3 combo/analysis tables (element assignment only where Gematsu/GameFAQs verified it, otherwise flag): Earth, Skuld (Juna default; Lv1 Needle Shoot, HP+400/EP+40/STR+20/DEF+20/ATS+155/ADF+100/SPD+4; ability: high HP increases Break damage, up to +80%), Keeper (Machias, Earth), Minotauros (Earth, per combo table); Water, Canon, Kaleido, Virgo, Oberon (names seen, element UNVERIFIED from my reads); Fire, Regulus (STR/DEF up, more CP on taking damage, S-Craft cooldown halved at higher level), Aegis (Rean default in CS3, note: Aegis was Earth in CS2, Fire in CS3 per GameFAQs CS3 table, flag this element change as needing a second check, do not present as settled), Brigid, Beowulf; Wind, Sirius (SPD up, wind Arts, evasion counter hits several times normal damage), Zephyr, Tempest, Chevalier; Time, Thor, Deva, Katze, Gungnir (names seen, element UNVERIFIED); Space, Dunamis, Scorpio, Sophia, Pandora (names seen, element UNVERIFIED); Mirage, Magius, Titania, Emblem, Moebius (names seen, element UNVERIFIED).

## 4. Arts-related items

- EP Charge I / II / III / IV, consumable, restores EP in battle/field.
  - CS1: EP Charge I restores 100 EP (500 mira), EP Charge II 300 EP (1,500 mira), verified from Roer shops.
  - CS3: EP Charge I 60 EP (200 mira), II 180 EP (800 mira), III 540 EP (3,200 mira), IV (value UNVERIFIED, seen in treasure lists, no shop price/effect in lines read). Values change per game, do not mix CS1 and CS3 numbers.
- U-Material, key material, not a consumable. Used for: quartz R/SR upgrades, stat quartz tier upgrades, weapon upgrades (CS1: 7x U-Material per weapon upgrade, plus 700 mira), special quartz Trade Shop recipes (x2-16). Obtained from: monster drops (e.g. Bloody Fang, Diver Bit), chests (x3/x5/x10), quests, NG+ chests. No vendor sells U-Material in the sources read, flag as UNVERIFIED if a shop sells it.
- Sepith, see synthesis above. Elemental sepith (Earth/Water/Fire/Wind/Time/Space/Mirage) + "All Sepith" bundles from chests. Also obtained from attacks/crafts with Kaleido / Scepter accessories (CS1 Scepter: "Obtain sepith from attacks/crafts", 2,000 mira).
- Charging Stations / Orbment Charging Stations, free, in the world, not an item. In keeps/dungeons (Einhel Keep, etc.) they restore HP and EP free. CS3 guide: "Use the Orbment Charging Station++ to restore your HP/EP before facing the boss." There is no "EP Charge station item" to carry, it is a place. Git-FED already picked this in Part 3, correctly as a place.
- Moon Lens, quartz/accessory, verified: lets you see data on enemies you have not scanned, and see chests/enemy locations on the map. CS3: reward from Einhel Keep Trial LV2 quest (Professor Schmidt, 3,000 mira + Moon Lens). This is the Arts/Quartz-adjacent item Git-FED already picked, do not lose it.
- Arts-boosting accessories (not quartz, but tied to Arts use): Pandora (offensive Arts damage up, more EP consumed, CS1 16,000 mira / CS3 8,000 mira), Kaleido (sepith from attacks/crafts), Oberon (prevents ailments/stat-down), Gloom (attacks/crafts inflict random ailments), Beowulf (offensive Arts increase CP), Moebius / Criminal (increases potency of healing items), Wing (prevents abnormal status/stat-down), Scepter (sepith from attacks/crafts), Megalith (increases EXP). These are accessories, not items, if FED-13 items are split character/class/role/creature/monster, these belong in character accessories, not in the Quartz list.
- Craft/Arts CP items: Impassion / Heat Up are Arts, not items. No "CP Charge" consumable was found in the sources read, flag: CP-restoring items were not verified; do not invent one.

## 6. Other systems tied to Arts / Quartz

- Orbment slots and lines (ARCUS / ARCUS II): Each character has a unique orbment configuration, slots connected by lines. Opening a slot increases max EP; longer connected lines = larger EP increase on opening. CS3 ARCUS II: 7 regular quartz slots + 2 Master Quartz slots (Main + Sub), trading one regular slot for the Sub slot vs CS1/CS2 (8 regular + 1 Master, UNVERIFIED exact CS1 slot count, flag). Slot layout varies per character and affects fighting style.
- Quartz Set Effect: The combined effect of set quartz is shown lower-left on the quartz screen, stats/passives from the set, not just individual quartz.
- Elemental values / line bonus: Slotting quartz of an element raises that element's value in the orbment; reaching thresholds unlocks Arts (in older Trails) / in CS, the Art comes from the quartz or Master Quartz itself. Do not mix the two models, in CS1-3, Art quartz and Master Quartz grant Arts directly; there is no separate "elemental value unlock" list verified in CS1-3.
- Arts cast time / delay: Every Art has Cast (time until it fires) and Delay (time until next turn). Bell quartz halves delay for that element. Cast quartz (Mind/Cast) reduces cast time, values UNVERIFIED.
- EP: Max EP rises by opening orbment slots and by EP quartz / Master Quartz EP boosts. EP is spent per Art, restored by EP Charge items, charging stations, and passive quartz (Ingenuity, Vulcan Gem, Dragon Vein, gradual EP recovery on field).
- Brave Points / Brave Orders, Break, Unbalanced: Already picked by Git-FED, not Quartz items, do not re-file them here.
- Weapon upgrades with U-Material: Already in section 4, U-Material is the shared currency between Quartz upgrades and weapon upgrades. That shared sink is a system Git-FED may want to keep: one material, two upgrade paths.
- Orbal Factories / Workshops: The shop type that does quartz synthesis, quartz upgrades, weapon upgrades, and (CS3 Training Grounds) sells consumables. In FED-13 terms, this is the Puffkin shop / synthesis shop role, already picked, but the Trails name for it is Orbal Factory, and it also does weapon upgrades, not just quartz.
