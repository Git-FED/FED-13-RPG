Rebuilt 2026-11-22. Feral entries only. All 14 Ivalice races are Bondkin, not monsters, so no Bondkin bloodline appears in this file. The 5 core bloodlines and the 9 other Bondkin bloodlines live in new-game.md and the Bondkin advancement file, not here.

Rule: no basic animals that can be found on planet Earth. Only fantasy creatures and monsters.
Roster: 24 in The Bank, deploy 6. Warden cap 150, Bondkin cap 200.
Graphics: Level 3, 256x256, HD 2D. Sprites: front, back, left, right, for everything.
