# The Codex - Renewed, No Earth Animals, Expanded With MMO Monsters

New game, name TBD. Bondkin are trained partners. Feral are wild monsters you catch later.
Rule: no basic animals that can be found on planet Earth.
Only fantasy creatures, monsters, and humanoids.
Roster: 24 in The Bank, deploy 6. Warden cap 150, Bondkin cap 200.
Graphics: Level 3, 256x256, HD 2D. Sprites: front, back, left, right, for everything.

## Notes

- All monsters are fantasy only, no basic Earth animals.
- Monsters are Feral, wild, catchable later, add to The Bank, 24 roster, 6 deployed.
- Bondkin are trained partners, not these monsters, monsters become Bondkin after you catch and train them.
- Hollow and Echo are regional variants, not shiny. Hollow = dark, corrupted, shadow. Echo = ghostly, pale, faded.
- Total now: 195 entries, 5 Bondkin core, 190 Feral monsters.
