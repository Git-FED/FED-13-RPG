## 1. The one sentence

Bondkin do NOT evolve into a different species. A Bondkin advances, in 5 stacking tracks, inside its own bloodline, and it can change sub race or clan, like a job change. Species name root never changes. Codex #001-#005 never changes. Bloodline never changes.

Character, Bondkin, and Monsters are three separate things. Character is you, the player. Bondkin is your partner creature, your starter, caught and trained. Monsters are Feral, wild, catchable later. A monster becomes a Bondkin after catch and train. Character cap 150, Bondkin cap 200.

## 3. The 5-track Advancement system, single coherent system

All 5 tracks stack. A Bondkin can be, at the same time, an Elder stage, Ascended Path, Surge Form, Hollow variant, Totem Form user, in a changed sub clan. No track changes bloodline or Codex number.

### Track 1: Growth Stage, within bloodline only
Young 0.7 / Adult 0.85 / Elder 1.0, same name root.
- Young: child version, smallest, unlock at catch / Lv 1
- Adult: unlock Lv 25-35, or Bond Lv 5+
- Elder: unlock Lv 60-80, or Bond Lv 5+ with a second condition, Git-FED to confirm which
- Evolution is a choice, not automatic.
- Feral monsters still evolve Stage 1-3 after catch, as a Bondkin. Wild Feral do not evolve before being caught.
- Children versions exist, so Feral species do technically evolve child to adult to elder within the same species.

Decision: Growth Stage, stats vs art only.
DECIDED: Stats AND art. Growth Stage changes art, size, and stats, small stat growth, not a species change. It is not art only, because a Growth Stage that only changed art would be invisible in battle numbers, and Git-FED's Feral evolution chains were built on Stage 1 / 2 / 3 with size and power growth. If Git-FED wants art only, he can override this, it is flagged as a decision made here, not a Git-FED pick.

Bondkin jobs are FFTA jobs, kept all and expanded. Not MMORPG classes. MMORPG classes go to the main character only, not Bondkin.

- Job change any time outside battle, from Party / Status menu, no penalty, no level loss, FFTA rule.
- Advanced / Expert jobs unlock by mastering 1-5 A-Abilities in a lower job, abilities learned from equipment with AP and stay learned.
- One ability and one skill per Bondkin equipped at a time, one passive and one active, AP to equip, every skill use takes a turn. Learn rule is separate: learn ALL action, reaction, support, combo, movement abilities from weapons / accessories.
- Race determines which jobs are available, base stats tendencies, zodiac and sex, per Part 4.
