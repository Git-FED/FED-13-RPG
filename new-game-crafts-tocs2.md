# FED-13 RPG - Crafts, Trails of Cold Steel 2

Source: Kiseki Wiki, List of crafts (Cold Steel II), read in full, lines 0-1265, crawled 2026-11-22. CS1 comparison from Kiseki character gameplay pages (Rean, Sara, Laura) and GameFAQs/Neoseeker CS2 guides for S-Break rules. No crafts invented.
Audit-only, no files edited.

Reading note, flag first: Kiseki labels the cost column "QD". Every other source (GameFAQs, Neoseeker, Kiseki character pages) calls the same gauge CP (Craft Points). I treat QD = CP throughout. Do not mix the two labels in FED-13 files.

Counts: 25 sections, 193 table rows, about 187 unique names (difference is duplicates: Rean's 2 S-Crafts listed twice, once normal and once under Spirit Unification; Valimar's Lustrous Slash / Flame Impact / Flame Dragon / Heavenly Slash listed twice, once as mech skills and once under Awakener's Call). Every playable character has 2 S-Crafts, except Lloyd, Rixia, Alfin, Crow, Vita, Altina who have 1 each. Valimar and Ordine have no CP S-Crafts, they have BP Finishers instead.

System rules (all verified):
- Gauge drained is CP for almost everything. CP builds by attacking and taking damage. Regular Crafts cost 5-110 CP, most 20-50 CP. S-Crafts cost 100+ CP.
- S-Craft: 100+ CP. At 200 CP damage x1.5, some gain extra effect/range (e.g. Alfin Sacred Circle adds Art Reflect at 200 CP; Elliot Remedy Oratorio adds HP Regen 50%/2t at 200 CP).
- S-Break: press Triangle at 100+ CP to fire an S-Craft at any time, interrupting turn order. Afterward the user suffers only the S-Craft's normal delay (40 AT), discarding prior delay. GameFAQs/Neoseeker warn it adds heavy delay, use with caution.
- Exceptions to CP-only: Gaius Wild Rage / Wild Rage 2 drain HP (30% / 50% max HP) to restore CP (+60 / +90). Rean Awakener's Call drains 500 EP, not CP, to summon Valimar for 3 turns. Valimar/Ordine Finishers drain BP (Brave Points), 3 or 5 BP, not CP.
- Character-specific only: ALL Crafts are character-specific. No shared learnable list. Shared names are different moves: Arc Slash (Rean / Crow / Ordine, different cost/effect), Blade Throw (Crow / Ordine), Detector (Emma / Elliot, with different upgrades Detector-alpha / Detector-beta / Detector-gamma), Flame Impact / Flame Dragon (Rean / Valimar).

## New in CS2 vs CS1, pattern, not a full CS1 list
- No CS1 Craft was removed. Base CS1 Crafts return, usually at Initial in CS2, often rebalanced: e.g. Rean Gale 35 CP in CS1 to 45 CP in CS2; Laura Radiant Wings 30 CP in CS1 to 20 CP in CS2, and its effect changed (CS1 STR/DEF+50%, Insight, ATS/ADF-50%; CS2 STR+50% / ADF-50% / Insight only).
- Upgrade tiers added in CS2: "True" versions, "EX" versions, and "2" versions, unlocked at high levels (mostly Lv 46-114, some Lv 130-137). Examples: Rean True Autumn Leaf Cutter Lv46, True Arc Slash Lv68, Gale EX Lv81, Flame Dragon Lv95; Sara Thundering Roar EX Lv78, Thunder Vortex Lv89, Flash EX Lv114; Laura Radiant Blade Lv71, True Earth Cleaver Lv87, Radiant Spin Lv114; Alisa Flamberge 2 Lv77, Blessed Arrow 2 Lv101, Molten Storm Lv113.
- Second S-Craft per main character: Rean Termination Slash - Dawn (Finale, 425%, vs Azure Flame Slash 325%); Emma Zodiac Rain (Act 2, 425% vs Lord Albireon 325%); Sara Northern Limits (Act 2, 450% vs Plasma Storm 350%); Laura Radiant Lion (Lv105, 425% vs Radiant Waltz 325%); Alisa Radiance Arc (Lv89, 400% vs Judgment Arrow 300%); Machias Trinity Cannon (Lv98, 375% vs Ultimate Shot 275%); Jusis Asteion Knight (Lv98, 400% vs Crystal Saber 300%); Fie Shadow Brigade (Lv95, 425% vs Sylphid Dance 325%); Gaius Twin Cyclones (Lv78, 425% vs Calamity Hawk 325%); Millium Galactic Cannon (Lv82, 450% vs Gigant Breaker 350%); Elliot Remedy Oratorio (Lv79, full-heal S-Craft, vs Seventh Rhapsody).
- Spirit Unification (Rean only, Act 2, 100 CP): unlocks a whole second set, Fatal / Arcane versions, with the same two S-Crafts at higher power (425% / 500%).
- New playable characters in CS2 (no CS1 Craft list): Elise, Toval, Claire, Sharon, Angelica, Towa, Ordine, Lloyd, Rixia, Alfin, Crow, Vita, Altina. Crow was a boss/NPC in CS1; his CS2 set is a guest set.
- New systems in CS2: Valimar / Ordine Divine Knight crafts and BP Finishers / Unite Finishers; Overdrive is a separate system, not a Craft (restores 30% HP/EP/CP, 3 extra turns, instant Arts between linked pair, per GameFAQs CS2 guide).

## FED-13 takeaway for race Crafts
The CS2 template Git-FED asked for maps cleanly onto his 5-race rule: each race gets its own exclusive set, 3-4 base Crafts at 20-50 CP, support/recovery mixed in (not attack-only: every character has at least one support or recovery Craft), upgrades at high levels (True / EX / 2), and 1-2 S-Crafts at 100+ CP with the second unlocked late (Act 2 / Finale / Lv 78-137). The gauge is CP only, except the two deliberate exceptions above (HP-for-CP, EP summon, BP finishers).

## Flagged / unverified
- "QD" vs "CP": Kiseki CS2 list uses QD; all other sources use CP. Treat as same gauge, but verify against the game before locking numbers into FED-13 files.
- Acquire level shown as "**" in Kiseki, level not given: Machias Energy Shell 2, Elliot Resonant Beat, Fie Sweep 2. Do not quote a level for these.
- Cost/power drift between sources: CS1 vs CS2 numbers differ by design (see Rean Gale, Laura Radiant Wings above), and Kiseki vs GameFAQs occasionally differ on the same CS2 Craft. The table above follows Kiseki CS2 as primary; re-check any single number before using it as a FED-13 balance value.
- "New in CS2" for individual base Crafts: verified for the upgrade/second-S-Craft pattern and for the 13 new playable characters above. A complete CS1-by-CS1 name diff for every character was not fully cross-checked character by character beyond Rean, Sara, and Laura; do not claim a specific base Craft is CS2-new unless it is in the upgrade / new-character lists above.
