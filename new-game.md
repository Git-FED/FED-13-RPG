# Fed MMORPG

New game, name: Fed MMORPG.
No design, no build until Git-FED finishes picking features.
Assets first: create ALL assets before building anything. 4 directional sprites for every thing, front, back, left, right, plus battle front and back. Graphics: Level 3, 256 by 256, HD 2D, a lot more fur and shading.
100% own assets from scratch, no copyrighted assets, so everything can be sold.
Only rename copyrighted IP. Generic names stay original: Warrior, Paladin, Mage, Elf, Dwarf, Orc, etc.
Source games: Pokemon Emerald Hacked ROMs, Kingdom Hearts 2, Trails of Cold Steel 3, Undawn, Final Fantasy Tactics Advance.
Slack: #video-game. Daily visit about 09:11 EST.

## Main Character

Main character is you, the player.
You are not a Bondkin, you are not a monster.
Cap 150.
There is no race choice for the main character.

Class, character only:
You pick your class, then you go out and catch a Bondkin.
MMORPG classes go here, to the main character, not to Bondkin.
Flow: Class, Advanced Class, Twin Path, Twin Ascension.
Twin Path is Dual Profession: second character class at once, after Advanced Class.
Twin Ascension is Dual Dual Advancement: two advanced character classes at once.
Opening choice: Sword, Shield, Staff. It affects the main player.
World form changes: only for the main character, form changes everywhere it goes.
Crafts: character specific techniques costing CP. CP built by attacking and taking damage.
S-Crafts: super moves, 100+ CP, stronger at 200+ CP.
S-Break: press anytime at 100+ CP.

Character skills, from Undawn, not classes, not Bondkin jobs:
Logger, Miner, Gatherer, Hunter, Chef, Angler, Scavenger, Firearms Master, etc. 10+ roles.
Role XP: chop wood gains logger points, level up as logger to master logger one day.
Advanced skills are Master Logger, Master Miner, Master Gatherer, Master Hunter, Master Chef, Master Angler, Master Scavenger, Master Firearms Master.
Skills are completely separate from Bondkin jobs. They do not affect Bondkin, Bondkin jobs do not affect them.

Equipment, character:
Weapons, armor, shoes, accessories, upgradable with U-Materials.
Equipment slots exist. Soulblade system, Keyblade style, main player weapon, equippable and upgradable.
Costumes and accessory collecting.
New Game Plus: carry over equipment after clearing.

## Bondkin

Bondkin is what a main character catches. Bondkin is the same thing, your trained partner.
Your first Bondkin is your starter. The rest are partners, not starters.
It is not you, it is not a wild monster anymore once you catch it.
Cap 200.

Bondkin have a bloodline / race:
Hybrid, Puffkin, Elderkin, Harefolk, Drakescale. 5 core bloodlines.
Race determines which jobs are available and base stats.
Hybrid is human like, and Hybrid is not catchable. Humans are not to be caught and captured.
Bondkin are not the main character, and the main character has no race choice.

Path, job, Bondkin only, a character cannot pick these:
Bondkin jobs are FFTA jobs, kept all and expanded. Not MMORPG classes.
117 Paths total. MMORPG classes do not go here.
Tank, Healer, Melee, Caster, Ranged, and the rest, about 117 in full list, see new-game-bondkin-jobs.md.

Ascended Path, Bondkin only, 30 total:
Advanced FFTA jobs only, not MMORPG advanced classes.
Nightlord, Galerider, Grand Avatar, Shade Saint, Vampyr, and the rest of the 30.

Abilities, Bondkin:
Learn ALL action, reaction, support, combo, and movement abilities from weapons and accessories.
Equip only one ability and one skill at a time, one passive and one active, AP to equip. Every skill use takes a turn.

Evolution, Bondkin only:
Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, smallest to largest. Stage 1 = 0.7, Stage 2 = 0.85, Stage 3 = 1.0. No Mega.
Wild Feral do not evolve before being caught. Evolution only after catch, as Bondkin.
Hollow and Echo are regional variants, not evolutions, not shiny.
Hollow: dark, corrupted, shadow, Dark type added, stronger attack, weaker defense.
Echo: ghostly, pale, faded, Ghost type added, stronger defense and speed, weaker attack.
A Hollow or Echo variant carries through evolution, it is not an evolution itself.
Totem Spirits: own version of Totema, keep all.

Roster: 24 in The Bank, deploy 6.
If all six deployed are killed you are not auto sent to healing, you may choose other party members if they are available.
The Bank is Monster Bank plus daycare. Growth items, take it. Link trading later.

Monsters and Bondkin share the same species list. A monster becomes a Bondkin, it does not become a different species.

## Battle, World, Story, Transport, Systems

Battle: FFTA small grid turn based battles, 1v1 to 6v6, larger map for more creatures. 2.5D isometric DS style look, aim FFTA2 DS graphics. No random encounters, enemies visible on field. Field attacks and encounter advantage. AT bonuses, Link Attacks, Break system, Unbalanced status, status effects work on bosses too.
World themes: 12 disconnected worlds, not 15. Homestead, Guild and dispatch, and Feral wilds are systems and a catching area, not worlds. Cut all Disney, keep themes only, no Disney names, no alien, no space. Alien and space replaced by The Thirteen and their secrecy. Superhero multiverse replaced by magic multiverse.
Story: dialogue boxes with character portraits are extremely important, not very text heavy. Only 20 to 30 cutscenes, only 5 to 10 voiced scenes. Side NPC dialogue text only.
Transport: Sky Skiff, formerly Gummi Ship, for traversing faster, not a weapon. Rollerblades, skateboard, bike, boat, kayak, wagon, plus buy a horse to pull it. Sky Galleon, formerly Airship. Golden Strider, formerly Chocobo, rideable bird.
The Lattice, formerly ARCUS II Orbment: slot Shards and Core Shard to gain stats, passives, and Arts.
Grading: every item graded. F 0, E 1-21, D 22-51, C 52-79, B 80-99, A 100-139, S 140-169, SS 170-199, SSS 200-249, SSSS 250+.
Clan and dispatch: clan up to 24, deploy 6, clan titles, levels, skills, turf, clan battles for items. Dispatch and guild missions, favorite part, deploying for 5 to 10 to 20 days.
Judge and laws: tournaments only, not everywhere. Judge points, yellow card, red card, jail for the duration of the match or tournament only.
Undawn survival and homestead: survival indicators change by biome, hometown hunger slow, desert hunger and hydration faster, stinky area mood drops faster, poisonous swamp health drops, city mood drops a little faster. Hygiene is minor visual only, character gets dirtier until they shower. Homestead levels gate character level, Lv1 caps at 15, Lv2 unlocks to 25, farmland at Lv3, relocate at Lv2, upgrades at Homestead Sign.
