# 1 - Character

Character is you, the player. Not Bondkin, not a monster. Separate.
A character is what catches a Bondkin.
A character cannot be the same class as a Bondkin. That does not make sense.
Character classes and Bondkin jobs are completely different lists, no overlap.

## Character Creation, In Order

1. Race
- Hybrid, Puffkin, Elderkin, Harefolk, Drakescale.
- Hybrid is human like, not Human, no human type.

2. Character Class, Pick Before Bondkin
- Pick 1 character class. These are character only, Bondkin cannot pick these.
- Catcher - catches Bondkin and monsters, best catch rate.
- Tamer - trains Bondkin, bond up faster.
- Trainer - trains Bondkin, XP up faster.
- Explorer - finds new areas, maps, treasure.
- Scout - finds wild monsters, sees them first.
- Merchant - buys and sells, best prices.
- Chef - cooks, buff food.
- Angler - fishes, finds water monsters.
- Miner - mines stone and ore.
- Logger - chops wood.
- Gatherer - harvests plants.
- Scavenger - finds items.
- Healer - heals Bondkin outside battle.
- Smith - makes weapons and armor.
- Scholar - learns, Codex, enemy data.

3. Advanced Character Class
- Unlock later by level and story.
- Catcher to Master Catcher to Grand Catcher.
- Tamer to Master Tamer to Grand Tamer.
- Explorer to Master Explorer to Grand Explorer.
- Merchant to Master Merchant to Grand Merchant.
- Chef to Master Chef to Grand Chef.

4. Twin Path, Character
- Dual profession, learn a second character class at once, after Advanced Class.
- Example: Catcher + Tamer, Explorer + Merchant.

5. Twin Ascension, Character
- Dual dual advancement, two advanced character classes at once.

## Character Only

- Level cap 150.
- A character catches a Bondkin.
- A character catches monsters, and a monster becomes a Bondkin after you catch and train it.
- Character classes are NOT Bondkin jobs. No Warrior, no Mage, no Paladin, no Thief for the character. Those are Bondkin only.
