Warden training board

Pick your
calling.

Character classes belong to the warden. Bondkin have their own Paths. Keep the two lists separate and everybody has a much better day.

Field / Catching

Catcher

Best catch rate, suspiciously good at throwing things under pressure.

Field / Discovery

Explorer

Finds new areas, maps treasure, and always knows where camp is.

Craft / Provision

Chef

Turns ingredients into buffs and arguments about who gets the last bite.

Craft / Gear

Smith

Makes weapons and armor. Can tell a good workbench from a decorative table.

Knowledge / Codex

Scholar

Learns enemy data, documents everything, and says “actually” a lot.

Support / Camp

Healer

Heals Bondkin outside battle and keeps the potion budget from exploding.

Progression

One class is a start.
Not a cage.

Unlock an Advanced Class, then open a Twin Path to learn a second profession at once. Twin Ascension takes that idea and makes it delightfully excessive.

Advanced ClassUnlock through level and story
Twin PathDual profession after advancement
