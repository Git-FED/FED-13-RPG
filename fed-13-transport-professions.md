# FED-13 Transport Professions

Rule from Git-FED, 2026-11-22: you cannot use any transportation item without the proper profession.

The game is set a little in the past, so all transport is period appropriate, horse and carriage is the model. No motor vehicles, no cars, no motorcycles, no helicopters, no mechs, no racers.

Four transport professions cover every transport tool. Each profession has 5 levels. Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr, Lv 2 costs 2,000 Cr, Lv 3 costs 4,000 Cr, Lv 4 costs 8,000 Cr, Lv 5 costs 16,000 Cr. Higher levels open more routes and better rewards.

## Professions

Pilot: flies air transport. Learned at a flight school, first at Saltquay. Lessons are a blacked out cut scene. License test: three questions, you get an item, then a 1v1 duel with the professor, wind style attacks and passive skills, extremely high evasiveness, the lesson is being extremely evasive. Grants the Pilot's License.

Sailor: sails water transport. Learned at a sailing school, first at Saltquay. Lessons are a blacked out cut scene. License test: three questions, you get an item, then a 1v1 duel with the sailing master, water style attacks and passive skills, extremely high defense, the lesson is weathering the storm. Grants the Sailor's License.

Rider: casual personal transport. Learned at the skate park in Saltquay, you can become a skateboarder, stuff like that. It does not have to be that serious for the Rider part. There is no formal riding school, and no riding school in Barleyford. There is no quiz and no duel. The Rider test is a short run at the skate park: land 3 tricks on the kick scooter, or ride the park loop on roller skates, or finish a hobby horse lap without falling. Pass the run and you get the Rider's License. Grants the Rider's License.

Coachman: drives horse drawn vehicles and handles horses and mounts. Replaces the Driver profession, which was for motor vehicles and no longer fits. Learned at a coachman school at the Saltquay carriage yard. Lessons are a blacked out cut scene. License test: three questions, you get an item, then a 1v1 duel with the driving master, earth style attacks and control passives, the lesson is control of the horses. Grants the Coachman's License.

## Transport list

1. Roller skates: Rider, Rider's License, learned at the skate park, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr. Was rollerblades, now period roller skates with wooden wheels and leather boots.
2. Kick scooter: Rider, Rider's License, learned at the skate park, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr. Was skateboard, now a wooden push scooter.
3. Hobby horse: Rider, Rider's License, learned at the skate park, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr. Was bike, now a wooden dandy horse push bike with no pedals.
4. Boat: Sailor, Sailor's License, learned at the sailing school, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr. Covers the built boat and Undawn boats.
5. Kayak: Sailor, Sailor's License, learned at the sailing school, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr.
6. Wagon plus horse: Coachman, Coachman's License, learned at the coachman school, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr. The horse is free at clan camps or bought at a stable for 500 Cr.
7. Riding horse: Coachman, Coachman's License, learned at the coachman school, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr. Was motorcycle, now a lone horse mount. Bought at a stable for 500 Cr or caught wild and tamed.
8. Golden Strider: Coachman, Coachman's License, learned at the coachman school, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr. Caught and bred, not built.
9. Sky Skiff: Pilot, Pilot's License, learned at the flight school, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr.
10. Sky Galleon: Pilot, Pilot's License, learned at the flight school, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr. Build it halfway through the game or buy one for 50,000 Cr, either way you still need the license to fly it.
11. Carriage: Coachman, Coachman's License, learned at the coachman school, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr. Was sedan, now a standard horse drawn carriage.
12. Gig: Coachman, Coachman's License, learned at the coachman school, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr. Was sports car, now a light fast two wheeled carriage.
13. Trail cart: Coachman, Coachman's License, learned at the coachman school, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr. Was off-road vehicle, now a rugged cart for rough terrain.
14. Freight wagon: Coachman, Coachman's License, learned at the coachman school, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr. Was truck, now a heavy hauling wagon.
15. Armored carriage: Coachman, Coachman's License, learned at the coachman school, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr. Was homemade armored vehicle, now an armored war carriage.
16. Horse buggy: Coachman, Coachman's License, learned at the coachman school, first at Saltquay, Lv 1 costs 1,000 Cr. Was buggy, now a light horse drawn buggy.

## Cut, no period equivalent

Epsilon Machro Bike, Emerald Legacy Bike Upgrade, Explorer, Helicopter, ATV, Jeep, SUV, Monster Truck, Futuristic Mech, Grand Prix Racer. These 10 are cut. Their roles are covered by the 16 that remain: the gig covers fast racing, the trail cart covers off-road, the wagon plus horse covers utility hauling, the Sky Skiff and Sky Galleon cover air travel.

## Notes

The 8 Undawn event and promo names (Windwraith Mercury, Storm Fantasy, Galaxy Red Emperor, Red Gem Meteor, Windwraith Azure, Aurora Wings, Thunderstorm, Heart of Steel) are skins, not separate vehicles. Each uses the profession of the base vehicle it skins. A skin whose base was cut is cut too.

Story only transport (Trails trains, the Courageous, the Derfflinger, ferries) is scripted and needs no profession. The profession rule covers transport tools the player owns and uses.
