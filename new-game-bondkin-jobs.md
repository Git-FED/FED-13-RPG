# Bondkin Jobs - All Jobs A Bondkin Can Learn

Bondkin = trained partners. A Bondkin has a bloodline, a class, an advanced class, then a Twin Path, then a Twin Ascension.
Jobs are called Paths. Advanced jobs are called Ascended Paths.
117 Paths, 30 Ascended Paths.
Abilities are learned from equipment, with AP, like FFTA. Buy a weapon to learn a skill.

## How jobs work

- Bloodline decides which Paths are available, and base stats.
- Race: Hybrid, Puffkin, Elderkin, Harefolk, Drakescale, plus pool bloodlines.
- Class: pick 1 at start.
- Advanced Class: unlock by level and story.
- Twin Path: dual profession, learn a second Path at once.
- Twin Ascension: dual dual advancement.
- One ability and one skill per Bondkin, one passive and one active, every skill use takes a turn.
- AP and abilities: abilities cost AP to equip.
- Level caps: Hybrid 150, Bondkin 200.

## Notes

- Bondkin do NOT have all MMORPG classes. Bondkin are different versions of FFTA creatures, own versions, not the names.
- Humans are NOT catchable. Hybrid is human like, not catchable.
- Jobs are learned from equipment. Buy a weapon, learn its skill. Steal to learn.
- Ultima attacks, Totem Spirits, all included.
- Grading: F 0, E 1-21, D 22-51, C 52-79, B 80-99, A 100-139, S 140-169, SS 170-199, SSS 200-249, SSSS 250+.
- Every item is graded.
