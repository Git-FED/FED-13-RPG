# FED-13 - Crafts Diff, Cold Steel 1 to 2 to 3

Built 2026-11-22 from the Kiseki Wiki crafts lists for Cold Steel, Cold Steel II, and Cold Steel III. All 21 unverified items resolved 2026-11-22, see new-game.md Decisions section. No em dashes. CP costs are CP. FED-13 uses the CS3 Craft sets as its base, see new-game.md.

## Global patterns

CS1 to CS2: every returning member keeps the CS1 base set, now all Initial instead of level gated. Durations mostly cut from 4 to 5 turns to 3 turns. Many status chances cut. Each member gains level gated upgrades (True, EX, or numbered 2 versions) and a second S-Craft.
CS2 to CS3: full rework with the Break stat added. Returning members are trimmed to 3 to 4 crafts plus 1 S-Craft. No EX or numbered 2 upgrades remain in CS3. Five new playable members join.
