# FED-13 RPG - Crafts, FFTA Race-Specific Abilities

For FED-13 RPG per-bloodline Craft list. Audit-only, no files edited. No abilities invented.

## How FFTA actually works — critical gauge distinction

FFTA has no Crafts and no CP gauge. That is Trails, not FFTA:

- AP (Ability Points) = LEARN cost only, not use cost. Equip the named weapon/armor/accessory, earn AP in battles / dispatch missions, master the ability at 100 / 200 / 300 / 999 AP. AP is not spent to use the ability in battle.
- MP = USE cost. Most A-Abilities cost MP in battle; physical techs often cost 0 MP, magic costs MP. I did not verify MP per ability for most jobs in the sources read — see Unverified section. The one job page I did read with an MP column was Gladiator: Rush/Wild Swing/Beatdown/Blitz = no MP, Fire/Bolt/Ice Sword = 10 MP, Ultima Sword = 60 MP.
- JP (Judge Points) = Totema + Combo cost. Totema uses all JP. Combo abilities use JP to attack with clan members.
- Trails Crafts (for comparison): character-specific attacks, cost CP, built by attacking / taking damage. S-Crafts cost 100+ CP, stronger at 200+ CP, S-Break can be used anytime at 100+ CP. That CP-gauge model is not in FFTA.

FED-13 Craft proposal implied by the sources: take the FFTA race/job-exclusive attack A-Abilities below as the Craft pool, but drain a Trails-style gauge (CP, or a FED-13 Craft gauge) on use, not FFTA MP/AP. That gauge choice is a FED-13 decision, not an FFTA fact.

## Job counts

42 race-job slots, 34 unique jobs:
- Human/Hybrid: 11 jobs
- Moogle/Puffkin: 8 jobs (Thief, Black Mage, White Mage shared; Animist, Mog Knight, Gunner, Juggler, Gadgeteer, Time Mage — Time Mage also Nu Mou)
- Nu Mou/Elderkin: 8 jobs
- Viera/Harefolk: 8 jobs, the only race with a tertiary tier (Assassin, Summoner)
- Bangaa/Drakescale: 7 jobs, all 7 exclusive to Bangaa

Shared jobs: White Mage (Human, Viera, Nu Mou, Moogle), Black Mage (Human, Nu Mou, Moogle), Thief (Human, Moogle), Archer (Human, Viera), Illusionist (Human, Nu Mou), Time Mage (Nu Mou, Moogle).

Sources: Final Fantasy Wiki — Final Fantasy Tactics Advance action abilities, Final Fantasy Tactics Advance jobs; Thonky FFTA jobs; RPGClassics NeoBlitz Ability FAQ v2.0 (23 Apr 2004); UPC Archive FFTA Jobs page. AP + teaching equipment are from RPGClassics / UPC, which agree on the values listed below. Effects are from RPGClassics where stated; where no effect was in the sources read, I give name / AP / weapon only and mark effect UNVERIFIED.

## 7. Movement Abilities — flag
FFTA (GBA) has no separate Movement ability slot / list in the sources read. Jobs list Move / Jump as stats (e.g. Soldier Move 4 Jump 2, Ninja Move 4 Jump 3, Assassin Move 4 Jump 4). Movement-related effects appear as Support / Reaction abilities (e.g. Monkey Grip, Doublehand, Shieldbearer) or as A-Ability effects (e.g. Speedbreak, Chrono / Haste). Do not invent a Movement ability list for FFTA. If FED-13 needs Movement Crafts, that is a FED-13 invention, not an FFTA pull.

## 9. Unverified / do not use as fact
- MP cost per A-Ability for all jobs except Gladiator (above). The UPC / RPGClassics sources give AP + teaching equipment, not MP. Do not assign MP values from memory.
- Effects for many A-Abilities marked UNVERIFIED above — names / AP / weapons are verified, effects are not in the sources read.
- Blue Mage A-Ability AP / MP / learning monster per spell — not verified in this pass.
- Beastmaster instrument-to-Control mapping is overlapping / confusing in UPC (same ability from multiple instruments, e.g. Flan from Demon Bell and Glass Bell). Do not present a 1-to-1 instrument list as settled.
- Special / secret job AP / weapons (Judgemaster, Hermetic, Runeseeker, Biskmatar).
- Movement abilities — none found in FFTA GBA; do not invent.
- Any CP cost — FFTA has no CP. Trails CP values (20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / 100+ CP) are a different system, researched separately.
- Totema MP/HP damage numbers — sources say "massive amount" only, no numbers verified.
- Spelling: Adrammelech vs Adrammalech — both appear in sources; Bangaa Totema is Adrammelech in most FF Wiki pages, Adrammalech in RPGClassics / action list. Flag, do not pick one as settled without a second check.
