# New Game - World Themes, 13 Worlds

Rebuilt 2026-11-22 to match the corrected design in new-game.md. 13 worlds: the Realm Map plus the 12 disconnected worlds. No Disney names, no Disney IP. No alien, no space, no superhero, no toys, no body swap, no digital grid.

Homestead, Guild and dispatch, and Feral wilds are NOT worlds. Homestead is a home base system. Guild and dispatch is a home base system. Feral wilds is the catching area where wild Feral live. The Lattice is a system, not a world.

Worlds are reached by Sky Galleon, inter-world travel only. The Sky Skiff is fast travel within one world, between its five towns and camps, and it cannot cross between worlds. Transport is not a weapon.

Each world has five towns, 65 towns total. Each world has one member of The Thirteen as its Trial Battle leader. Each disconnected world grants one world weapon. Duskhollow is the dark Hollow region. Lumenvale is the light Echo region.

World map size: every one of the 13 worlds has a map the size of a Pokemon game. Each world is a full region sized map, like a Pokemon region, not a small area. A Pokemon sized map means 5 towns per world, routes between the towns, wild areas with visible wild Beasts to catch, dungeons, landmarks, and a Trial Battle hall, and this scale applies to all 13 worlds equally, the Realm Map plus the 12 disconnected worlds.

## Realm Map, home continent

Realm Map
- Home continent, harbors, farms, mountains, oases, canals. Trial Battle 1.
- Towns: Saltquay, Cragfall, Barleyford, Dunewell, Reedbridge.
- Member: Maren Saltwright, harbormaster of Saltquay.
- Starting weapon: Soulblade.

## Group 1 - The Thirteen, secret organization

The Thirteen headquarters
- Sealed black stone citadel, secret meetings, dark grey coats, sealed masks.
- Towns: Vexhall, Umbragate, Hollowmoot, Nightcourt, Shroudmark.
  - Vexhall: A sealed black stone citadel town and seat of Aldous Vex, high steward of Vexhall, where masked stewards hold secret meetings behind high walls and grey coated couriers carry locked orders through torchlit streets.
  - Umbragate: A dark gate town at the shadowed entrance to the headquarters, where grey coated guards check sealed passes beneath a great black arch and torchlight never quite reaches the far wall.
  - Hollowmoot: A hollow hall town where masked delegates of The Thirteen gather to vote in silence, its echoing moot hall ringed by empty benches and locked side doors.
  - Nightcourt: A night trial town of black stone courts and shuttered windows, where masked judges hear sealed cases by lantern light and no verdict is ever spoken aloud in the street.
  - Shroudmark: A veiled market town where traders in grey coats and sealed masks sell locked boxes, coded ledgers, and letters with no sender, and every stall keeps its goods behind a drawn shroud.
- Member: Aldous Vex, high steward of Vexhall.
- World weapon: Vexbrand.

The Thirteen's dark corridors
- Shifting castle, white rooms, secret doors, memory loss, halls that change shape.
- Towns: Whitehollow, Doorwatch, Vellum, Corridale, Lostwick.
  - Whitehollow: A pale white stone town of bright empty rooms and secret doors, where the halls change shape overnight and pale robed keepers chalk new marks on the walls each morning.
  - Doorwatch: A town of door watchers who guard a hundred locked doors along one long hall, each watcher holding a ring of iron keys and a lantern that never goes out.
  - Vellum: A pale parchment town of scribes and map makers who draw the shifting halls on thin vellum sheets, their ink pots, drying racks, and scroll shelves filling every workshop.
  - Corridale: A town built along a single long corridor that bends and narrows without warning, its doors opening onto rooms that were not there the day before.
  - Lostwick: A winding lane town where travelers lose their way and their memories, its crooked streets lit by small blue lanterns that mark the only safe road home.
- Member: Selene Whitehollow, keeper of the white rooms.
- World weapon: Doorwarden Glaive.

The Thirteen's secrecy
- Secret organization, spies, hidden bases, coded messages, nobody knows who they really are.
- Towns: Cipherest, Whisperhold, Inkwatch, Veilmarket, Shadowpost.
  - Cipherest: A town of code makers and spymasters where cipher wheels turn in every workshop, sealed letters change hands in quiet courtyards, and no one speaks a plain word in public.
  - Whisperhold: A hushed town where all talk is done in whispers, its listening posts and quiet halls watched by hooded listeners who write down every soft word.
  - Inkwatch: A town of ink makers and message watchers where black ink, sealed scrolls, and carrier pigeons fill the workshops, and every letter is read twice before it leaves.
  - Veilmarket: A veiled market town where masked sellers trade in coded prices and wrapped goods, and buyers pay with sealed tokens behind curtains of dark cloth.
  - Shadowpost: A hidden post town of couriers and dead drops, where sealed satchels pass from hand to hand in shadowed lanes and no rider ever says where the next stop lies.
- Member: Orin Cipher, spymaster of Cipherest.
- World weapon: Whisperfang.

## Group 2 - Magic and mystical

Magic multiverse
- Magic multiverse, mystical portal, mystical world, all sorts of magic, portals, wizards, spells, magical realms.
- Towns: Portalwatch, Spellmere, Runestead, Wandwick, Arcanmere.
  - Portalwatch: A town guarding a great shimmering portal, where portal wardens in bright robes check every traveler and wizard towers ring the glowing gate.
  - Spellmere: A lakeside town where spells ripple across still water, its wizard towers, floating lights, and spell shops lining a shore that glows after dark.
  - Runestead: A town of carved runes and standing stones, where rune smiths cut glowing marks into rock and every doorway carries a ward against stray magic.
  - Wandwick: A town of wand makers where carved wood, polished cores, and wand shops fill the lanes, and apprentices test new wands in a fenced yard behind the guild hall.
  - Arcanmere: A deep magic town on a misty lake where arcane libraries, floating lanterns, and hooded scholars keep the oldest spells, and the water itself shimmers with stored light.
- Member: Thalia Spellmere, portal warden of Portalwatch.
- World weapon: Spellweave Staff.

Mind / emotions world
- Inside a person's head, inner landscape, emotions shape streets, halls, and weather.
- Towns: Joyford, Sorrowsong, Emberhold, Dreadhollow, Stillmere.
  - Joyford: A bright cheerful town of festivals, music, and sunny lanes, where laughter spills from every doorway and flower garlands hang across the streets.
  - Sorrowsong: A mournful town of grey rain and weeping willows, where singers in dark cloaks sing slow songs of loss and every window keeps a candle for the missing.
  - Emberhold: A town of burning hearths and forge fires where anger and warmth live side by side, its ember lit streets glowing red at dusk and its smiths working through the night.
  - Dreadhollow: A fearful town of dark fog and shuttered windows, where the streets whisper, the lamps burn low, and few folk walk out after the evening bell.
  - Stillmere: A calm town of still water and quiet halls, kept by Joren Stillmere, where the lake never ripples, the gardens never rustle, and every visitor speaks softly.
- Member: Joren Stillmere, keeper of Stillmere.
- World weapon: Empath Bow.

## Group 3 - Kingdoms

Fairy tale kingdom
- Fairy tale kingdom, forest, thorn walls, mirror halls, queen regent, poison apple orchards.
- Towns: Thornwick, Appleford, Mirrorhold, Forestgate, Grimmere.
  - Thornwick: A thorn walled town and seat of Rosalind Thornwick, queen regent of Thornwick, where rose gates guard a pale palace and thorn hedges ring every garden.
  - Appleford: An orchard town of red apple trees, cider presses, and market stalls, where the poison apple orchards stand fenced and locked beyond the sweet groves.
  - Mirrorhold: A town of mirror halls and polished glass, where every hall reflects its visitors twice over and mirror makers polish great silvered panels in sunlit workshops.
  - Forestgate: A gate town at the forest edge with timber walls, woodcutters, and hunters, where the road into the deep woods starts beneath a carved arch of oak.
  - Grimmere: A dark fairy tale town of crooked houses and grim lanes, where old tales are told by firelight and every lane ends at a door that should stay shut.
- Member: Rosalind Thornwick, queen regent of Thornwick.
- World weapon: Thornrose Rapier.

Hidden tech kingdom
- Hidden tech kingdom, gears, steam, wire, clocks, sealed mountain kingdom.
- Towns: Copperton, Gearford, Steamwick, Wirehold, Clockmere.
  - Copperton: A copper roofed town at the mountain foot where copper smiths hammer brass pipes and kettles, and green copper domes catch the sun above stone workshops.
  - Gearford: A town of great turning gears and workshops, seat of Ferran Gearford, chief engineer of Gearford, where clockwork lifts, brass bridges, and engine halls fill the valley.
  - Steamwick: A steam vent town of hissing pipes, bath houses, and engine halls, where white steam curls from every grate and brass gauges line the workshop walls.
  - Wirehold: A town of wire and cable where signal towers, brass instruments, and coiled wire fill the yards, and messengers send tapped signals from tower to tower.
  - Clockmere: A lakeside town of clocks and timekeepers, where clock towers ring the still water, brass clock faces mark every square, and the great town clock never stops.
- Member: Ferran Gearford, chief engineer of Gearford.
- World weapon: Geartooth Axe.

Lion kingdom, savanna
- Savanna, clan halls, strength trials, sun baked towns, king's story.
- Towns: Sunmane, Tallgrass, Goldmane, Kingwatch, Dustplain.
  - Sunmane: A sun baked town of golden roofs and clan banners, where the streets glow in the afternoon heat and clan elders meet beneath a great shaded awning.
  - Tallgrass: A grassland town of tall grass and thatch roofs, where hunters, herders, and grass cutters work the open plain and the wind bends the fields in waves.
  - Goldmane: A golden banner town and seat of Kito Goldmane, savanna lord of Goldmane, where the great clan hall stands at the center and strength trials are held in the dusty yard.
  - Kingwatch: A watch town guarding the king's road, where tall watch towers, trial grounds, and training yards line the route and guards drill from dawn to dusk.
  - Dustplain: A dusty plain town of dry wells, caravan rests, and open markets, where traders water their horses at the stone troughs and dust coats every roof.
- Member: Kito Goldmane, savanna lord of Goldmane.
- World weapon: Sunmane Spear.

Underwater kingdom
- Under the sea, reef kingdom, coral halls, pearl markets, deep trenches.
- Towns: Coralport, Tidehollow, Pearlwick, Deepmere, Shellford.
  - Coralport: A coral harbor town and seat of Nerissa Coralport, tide queen of Coralport, where coral halls rise from the reef, shell boats crowd the docks, and pearl lanterns light the water.
  - Tidehollow: A hollow reef town of tide pools and echoing caves, where the tide fills the lower streets twice a day and shell collectors work the pools at low water.
  - Pearlwick: A pearl market town of white stalls and pearl divers, where divers bring up gleaming shells, pearl cutters work at open benches, and buyers haggle over strands of white pearl.
  - Deepmere: A deep trench town of dark blue halls and glowing lanterns, where the water turns cold and still, and deep divers in brass helms walk the trench floor.
  - Shellford: A shell paved town where shell houses line a ford across the reef shallows, and carts cross the low water at ebb tide beneath strings of hanging shells.
- Member: Nerissa Coralport, tide queen of Coralport.
- World weapon: Tidecaller Trident.

## Group 4 - Water and island

Ocean island
- Ocean, island, voyaging, ocean magic, wayfinding, star paths.
- Towns: Wavecrest, Canoebay, Starpath, Tidereach, Palmford.
  - Wavecrest: A wave beaten cliff town and seat of Kai Wavecrest, wayfinder chief of Wavecrest, where a tall lookout watches the sea and voyaging canoes rest on the shingle below.
  - Canoebay: A bay town of canoes and paddlers, where fish drying racks line the sand, net makers mend sails, and every household keeps a canoe by its door.
  - Starpath: A town of star readers and wayfinders, where star charts cover the workshop walls, a night sky observatory crowns the hill, and sailors learn the star paths by heart.
  - Tidereach: A tidal town of long jetties and tide mills, where the mill wheels turn with the rising water and boats rise and fall against the wooden piers.
  - Palmford: A palm shaded town at a lagoon ford, where fruit stalls, woven mats, and resting voyagers crowd the shore, and the crossing is marked by carved palm posts.
- Member: Kai Wavecrest, wayfinder chief of Wavecrest.
- World weapon: Wavecrest Cutlass.

## Group 5 - Cities and towns

Animal city
- A period city with cobblestone streets, timber and brick buildings, horse drawn carts, constables, city jobs, detective work, and shared districts.
- Towns: Pawford, Burrowgate, Whiskerwick, Talltree, Denholme.
  - Pawford: A cobblestone city town and seat of Bramble Pawford, city warden of Pawford, where constables walk the main square, horse drawn carts fill the lanes, and timber and brick houses crowd the market.
  - Burrowgate: A low gate district of brick houses and burrow lanes, where cart drivers, porters, and night watchers live close to the city gate and the streets stay busy till late.
  - Whiskerwick: A market district of food stalls, bakeries, and shop signs, where the smell of fresh bread fills the lanes and traders call out their goods from dawn.
  - Talltree: A tall timber district of tree lined streets and high houses, where the tallest homes in the city rise above the lanes and laundry lines cross between the upper windows.
  - Denholme: A quiet home district of gardens, hearths, and low houses, where the city rests after dark and every lane ends at a small green with a well.
- Member: Bramble Pawford, city warden of Pawford.
- World weapon: Pawprint Hammer.

Haunted house
- Haunted mansion, ghosts, ghost hunting, mist, trials behind every door.
- Towns: Gravewick, Mistmoor, Hollowbell, Wraithford, Lanternfall.
  - Gravewick: A graveyard town and seat of Mortimer Gravewick, leader of The Thirteen, where the final Trial Battle is held, its leaning stones, iron gates, and cold chapel standing under a grey sky.
  - Mistmoor: A mist covered moor town where fog rolls across the lanes, lantern posts mark the road, and ghost hunters wait out the night in the shuttered inn.
  - Hollowbell: A bell tower town where hollow bells ring without a hand to pull them, its echoing streets lined with dark houses and a great cracked bell above the square.
  - Wraithford: A pale town of wraiths and cold light, where grey mist hangs over the ford, the houses stand white and still, and the river runs slow beneath a stone bridge.
  - Lanternfall: A lantern lit town where falling lantern light fills the streets, ghost hunters hang fresh lanterns at every door, and the old mansion waits at the top of the hill.
- Member: Mortimer Gravewick, leader of The Thirteen, final Trial Battle.
- World weapon: Gravebell Flail.
