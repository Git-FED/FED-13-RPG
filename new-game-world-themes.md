# New Game - World Themes, In Order Of Similarity, Reversed

All themes will be in the game. No Disney names, no Disney IP.
No alien, no space, no superhero, no toys, no body swap, no digital grid.
Grouped by similarity, most similar together, reversed, Group 1 on top, numbering reversed too.

## Group 1 - Our game systems, home base, on top

15. Homestead world
- Personal plot of land, build a base, walls, roofs, stairs, doors, decor, multi-storey, interior, farmland, storage, workbench.

14. Guild and dispatch world
- Guild missions, dispatch missions, story missions, turf missions, mission items, reports, deploying for 5 to 10 to 20 days.
- Similar to homestead world, both are home base systems, not a far away world, they are where you live and work.

## Group 2 - Cities and towns

13. Haunted house
- Haunted mansion, ghosts, ghost hunting.

12. Animal city
- Modern animal city, police, mammals, city jobs, detective work.
- Similar to haunted house, both are a place in a city / town, a building you enter.

## Group 3 - Water and island

11. Ocean island
- Polynesian ocean, island, voyaging, ocean magic, wayfinding.

## Group 4 - Kingdoms

10. Underwater kingdom
- Under the sea, mermaid kingdom, ocean, sea witch.
- Similar to ocean island, above, water world.

9. Lion kingdom, savanna
- Pride Lands, savanna, lions, animal kingdom, king's story.

8. Hidden tech kingdom
- Hidden tech kingdom, tech, water kingdom.

7. Fairy tale kingdom
- Fairy tale kingdom, forest, dwarfs, evil queen, poison apple.

## Group 5 - Magic and mystical

6. Feral wilds
- Wild areas, forests, routes, dungeons, where wild Feral live and you catch them.
- Replaces Lattice world, Lattice is a system, not a world, my mistake.
- Similar to magic multiverse, a wild mystical place.

5. Mind / emotions world
- Inside a person's head, emotions as characters, Headquarters, teen mind.
- Similar to magic multiverse, it is an inner mystical world.

4. Magic multiverse
- Magic multiverse, mystical portal, mystical world, all sorts of magic, portals, wizards, spells, magical realms.

## Group 6 - The Thirteen, secret organization, at the bottom

3. The Thirteen's secrecy
- Secret organization, spies, hidden bases, coded messages, nobody knows who they really are.

2. The Thirteen's dark corridors
- Dark corridors, white rooms, secret doors, memory loss, castle that changes.

1. The Thirteen headquarters
- Secret organization headquarters, black coats, dark corridors, secret meetings.
