# FED-13 RPG - What It Is

## Name

FED-13 RPG. FED for Git-FED, 13 for The Thirteen.
Repo: https://github.com/Git-FED/FED-13-RPG
Slack: #video-game

## What it is

A new RPG, built from feature teardowns of 5 games, so Git-FED can pick what he likes and hates from each. No design, no build of the game itself yet, assets first when design is approved.

Source games:
1. Pokemon Emerald, hacked ROMs, monster catching and evolution
2. Kingdom Hearts 2
3. Trails of Cold Steel 3, plus Arts / Quartz / Crafts from Trails 1 and 2
4. Undawn
5. Final Fantasy Tactics Advance, GBA

## Basic structure

Mankind, Bondkin, Beast. Nothing else.

- Mankind is the trainer, you, the player. Race: Polar or Solar. Cap 150.
  - Polar: the palest skin tones, from very pale European to light Asian and light Mediterranean / Spanish.
  - Solar: the deepest skin tones, from South Asian / Indian through darker Asian, Pacific Islander / Hawaiian, Black, and dark African.
- Bondkin is your partner creature, your starter, caught and trained. Cap 200.
- Beast is Feral, wild, catchable later. A Beast becomes a Bondkin after catch and train.

Creatures and Beasts get used like Pokemon in battle, but are treated different outside of battle.

- Bondkin do NOT have a Monster Bank. They have a daycare place in the game.
- Beasts are in The Bank, Monster Bank.
- Beasts can be bred like animals for eggs, like in Pokemon.
- Beasts can fuse, Pokemon Infinite Fusion style. Two Beasts fuse into one. Only Beasts fuse. Mankind and Bondkin cannot fuse. Routes, decided by Muse 2026-11-22: breeding (fused egg, head and body picked before hatching), daycare (fuse in place for 300 Crests), breeding items (Splicer, 300 Crests, Superior Splicer 1,000 Crests after four badges). A fused Beast can still become a Bondkin, yes. Fusion can be undone, yes, a Splicer unfuses and returns both Beasts, a Reverser swaps head and body for 300 Crests. A fused Beast cannot be fused again.
- Bondkin do not lay eggs. They have full children, that can have a high rarity, but it is triple the time it takes a Beast egg to be bred than hatched.

Items, gyms, NPCs, legendaries, etc. have not been discussed in this basic phase, except where they were already picked in Parts 1-5. Work is basic, slowly.

## 5 bloodlines

Bondkin bloodlines, from FFTA races, renamed:
1. Hybrid, human-like creatures, #001
2. Puffkin, Moogle, floating koala teddy bunny mixture
3. Elderkin, Nu Mou, old ancient sorcerers and wizards
4. Harefolk, Viera, humanoid bunny creatures
5. Drakescale, Bangaa, dragons, lizard dragons, dinosaur lizard dragons

Race determines which jobs are available and base stats tendencies. Zodiac and sex included.

Bondkin do NOT evolve into a different species. A Bondkin advances in 5 stacking tracks inside its own bloodline, and it can change sub race / clan, like a job change. Species name root never changes. Codex #001-#005 never changes. Bloodline never changes.

Tracks:
1. Growth Stage, Young 0.7 / Adult 0.85 / Elder 1.0, stats and art
2. Path to Ascended, FFTA verified only scope, 34 FFTA jobs plus 20 FFTA2 jobs, the 117 Paths and 30 Ascended totals are not adopted
3. Surge Form, see below
4. Hollow / Echo, regional variants, not evolutions
5. Totem Form, Totem Spirit attack, see below

Special / secret jobs are job evolutions, not species evolutions: Hermetic, Runeseeker, Judgemaster, Biskmatar, plus Ezel, Babus, Ritz, Shara, Cid, Eldena, Lini, Cheney, Pallanza, Quinn, Littlevilli.

## Battle

Two battle styles:
1. 1v1 is normal Pokemon style battles.
2. 2v2 and up to 6v6 is the FFTA small grid turn based team style battle, larger map for more creatures.

Same rules on both: 2.5D isometric DS style look, aim FFTA2 DS graphics, no random encounters, enemies visible on field, field attacks and encounter advantage, AT bonuses, Link Attacks, Break system, Unbalanced status, status effects work on bosses too.

Roster: clan size up to 24, deploy up to 6 per battle. If all six are killed you still lose, but you are not automatically sent to a healing area, you have the choice to use the other party members if they are available.

## Surge Forms

Drive Forms renamed. Every Bondkin has HP and MP bars. Surge Gauge fills with drive orbs / taking damage / attacks. The Surge Gauge resource is called Aether, decided by Muse 2026-11-22. Drive orbs are field only, small restores one tenth of one Surge bar, large restores one quarter of one Surge bar. Transforming fully refills HP and MP.

Final forms, in order:
1. Gale Form - Quick Run and Air Slide
2. Aegis Form - Dodge Roll
3. Zephyr Form - Glide
4. Tempest Form - Aerial Dodge
5. Zenith Form - High Jump

Form levels 1-7, separate from character level. Sora learnable skills are form skills. When a Bondkin changes its form, it unlocks a new set of skills, also unlockable per form level.

Aether is the name of the Surge Gauge resource, decided by Muse 2026-11-22. Might and Bastion are dead names, do not use them.

Mega is a temporary battle form. Totem Spirit attack is a special attack usable while Mega evolved, or as a Z-move if Mega is not used for that Bondkin. Mega is the default, Z-move is the backup.

## Hollow / Echo

Instead of shiny Pokemon. Regional variants, not evolutions, variant carries through evolution.

- Hollow: dark, corrupted, shadow, Dark type added, stronger attack. Found in dark regions.
- Echo: ghostly, pale, faded, Ghost type added, stronger defense and speed. Found in light regions.
- Hollow regions and Echo regions are created places in the world: Hollow variants are found in dark Hollow regions, Echo variants are found in light Echo regions. Hollow region is Duskhollow, Echo region is Lumenvale, decided by Muse 2026-11-22.

## Crafts

Per race, exclusive, no Craft shared across races. 30 total, 20 base + 10 S-Crafts, 6 per race.

- Hybrid: Rush 20, Wild Swing 30, First Aid 30, Throw 20, S Ultima Shot, Saint Cross
- Puffkin: Mog Attack 20, Fireshot 20, Cuisine 40, Gil Toss 20, S Ultima Charge, Chocobo Rush
- Elderkin: Drain 20, Water 30, Control 40, Flare 40, S Ultima Blow, Giga Flare
- Harefolk: Swarmstrike 20, Fire Whip 20, Cure 30, Doubleshot 40, S Ultima Masher, Last Breath
- Drakescale: Jump 20, Fire Breath 40, Revive 40, Body Slam 40, S Ultima Sword, Wyrmkiller

Gauge: Trails CP. Regular 20-80 CP, S-Crafts 100+ CP, consume all CP, stronger at 200 CP, S-Break at 100+ CP, max CP 200, CP built by attacking and taking damage, lost on K.O.
Upgrades: True Lv 46, EX Lv 81, 2 Lv 114.

## Quartz / Lattice

Quartz / The Lattice system is for the CHARACTER, not Bondkin. All Arts are jobs for Bondkin, added to the FFTA job list. Skills are learned via items, and skills attach to weapons like FFTA: equip the weapon, learn the skill.

Core Shard is character-only. It does NOT determine Bondkin affinity.
U-Material is for only what it applies to: character quartz upgrades to R / SR, and character weapon upgrades. It does NOT apply to Crafts, Bondkin gear, or monsters.
EP Charge is the CS3 set: I 60 EP / 200 mira, II 180 EP / 800 mira, III 540 EP / 3,200 mira, IV restores all EP, treasure and events only, no shop price.

## World

- Hoenn map renamed The Realm Map, routes / dungeons / connections, Dex renamed The Codex.
- 65 towns, 5 per world, 8 location types.
- Disconnected worlds via Sky Skiff style travel, transport is NOT a weapon, transport is for traversing faster: rollerblades, skateboard, bike, boat, kayak, wagon plus buy a horse to pull it, Sky Skiff, Sky Galleon, Golden Strider.
- World themes: cut all Disney, keep themes only, 13 worlds, Realm Map plus 12 disconnected worlds, plus Homestead, Guild and dispatch, Feral wilds, and the Lattice as systems, not worlds, the themes are the names for now, towns carry the identity.
- The Thirteen: Organization 13 renamed, incredibly important, hidden manipulators. Headquarters, dark corridors, secrecy replace alien / space themes.
- No Earth animals. No basic animals that can be found on planet Earth in the game.
- No human type, it is called Hybrid.

## Story / visuals

- Dialogue boxes with character portraits is extremely important. Not very text heavy.
- Cutscenes: only 20-30 total. Voiced scenes: only 5-10.
- Graphics: Level 3, 256x256, HD 2D, a lot more fur and shading. 4 directional sprites per creature, character, NPC, and Feral, front, back, left, right, plus battle front and back.
- Battle look: 2.5D, 3D isometric maps with characters and objects based on 2D sprites, 16 bit throwback, Game Boy 2X style, aim Nintendo DS style, aim FFTA2 DS graphics.
- App images: 2D, not realistic, not cute.

## Key systems picked

- Gym battles renamed Trial Battles, progression signal.
- Trial Battles, Dex / Codex, physical / special split BY TYPE not by move.
- Heartless renamed Hollow, Nobodies renamed Echoes, Keyblade renamed Soulblade, Gummi Ship renamed Sky Skiff, Moogle renamed Puffkin, Drive Forms renamed Surge Forms.
- World weapons: every disconnected world grants a new weapon themed to that world, like Sora getting a new Keyblade in every world in Kingdom Hearts. Which weapon per world, decided by Muse 2026-11-22: Vexbrand (headquarters), Doorwarden Glaive (dark corridors), Whisperfang (secrecy), Spellweave Staff (magic multiverse), Empath Bow (mind and emotions), Thornrose Rapier (fairy tale kingdom), Geartooth Axe (hidden tech kingdom), Sunmane Spear (lion kingdom savanna), Tidecaller Trident (underwater kingdom), Wavecrest Cutlass (ocean island), Pawprint Hammer (animal city), Gravebell Flail (haunted house).
- Grimoire: an AI assisted in-game wiki. Four Grimoires are offered: Muse, Grok, the Wisp, and the Chronicler. The player can switch Grimoires at any Puffkin shop for 500 Crests, contacts and notes carry over. The character chooses one and it guides them through the game. Which guide you get depends on the opening choice: Sword gets a fairy, magic gets a mystical orb, and the in-between choice gets the Grimoire. The mystical orb and the fairy do the same job as the Grimoire: the wiki and the contacts. The Grimoire is FED-13's PokeNav, the in-game device like the PokeNav and other Pokemon devices. It is also a contact book: characters write each other's names in each other's Grimoires, like the PokeNav Match Call, so they can contact each other for a rematch. Any question the character has can be solved by their Grimoire. It looks like a mystical conversation, but it is just a generic AI chat loaded with answers about the game.
- Save points, own version. Puffkin shops selling items / weapons / armor / accessories and hosting synthesis, synthesis using collected items.
- Opening choice Sword / Shield / Staff affecting main player, equippable / upgradable weapons.
- Magic spells, effect only to help / hurt, healing spells given.
- Summons, different: quick attack from a party member, plus LINK system where two creatures linked in party, summoning attack summons its linked friend.
- Allies fight on their own AI. Guest parties. Legendary creatures can be rescued, King Mickey rescue only for legendaries.
- AP and abilities, abilities cost AP to equip. One ability and one skill per creature, one passive and one active, every skill use takes a turn.
- Level caps: character 150, creatures 200.
- Fast travel between visited locations from map menu, unlockable way later, maybe after 7th gym. New Game Plus, carry over equipment after clearing.
- AT battle, turn based battles in separate arena, AT bonuses, attack affinities, Link Attacks, Unbalanced and Combat Links, Arts with cast time and delay, Crafts, CP, S-Crafts 100+ CP, stronger at 200+ CP, S-Break, Brave Points, Break system, status effects work on bosses too, field attacks, no random encounters.
- Homestead, take all, double expanded if possible. Personal plot, build a base, walls / roofs / stairs / doors / decorative furnishing / multi-storey / interior, homestead levels gate character level, Lv1 caps at 15, Lv2 unlocks to 25, relocate at Lv2, farmland at Lv3. Backpack limited, homestead cabinets store extra. Gathering, chop wood, mine stone / ore, harvest plants, hunt animals and fish. Crafting, all of it.
- Undawn survival by biome: Hometown hunger slow, Desert hunger and hydration faster, Stinky area mood drops faster, Poisonous swamp health drops, City mood drops a little faster. Food / water is extra, not a focus. Hygiene is minor visual, dirtier until shower.
- Undawn roles are character skills, not classes: Logger, Miner, Gatherer, Hunter, Chef, Angler, Scavenger, Firearms Master, etc. Role XP, chop wood gains logger points, level up as logger to master logger.
- Clan system: clan titles, levels and skills, clan turf, clan battles, territory, for items. Dispatch and guild, favorite part: story missions, guild missions, dispatch system, turf missions, mission items, reports, deploying for 5 to 10 to 20 days.
- Judge and laws, tournaments only: Judge points, yellow card, red card, jail, jail only means contest jail, for the duration of the match or tournament.
- Totem Spirits, own version of Totema, one per race, guardian move, names approved by Muse 2026-11-22: Hybrid Dawnwarden, Puffkin Brightpom Sovereign, Elderkin Quiet Flame Sage, Harefolk Moonhare Matriarch, Drakescale Emberwyrm King.
- Monster Bank, daycare, growth items, link trading later.
