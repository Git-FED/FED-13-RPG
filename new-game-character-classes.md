# Character Classes - Pick Before Your Bondkin Starter

Order: you pick your character first, then you pick your Bondkin starter.
Character creation is Race, Class, Advanced Class, then Bondkin starter, then Twin Path, then Twin Ascension.

## Step 1 - Race, Your Bloodline

Pick 1 of 5 core bloodlines:
Hybrid - human like, balanced, all special jobs, not catchable.
Puffkin - floating koala teddy bunny, magic, shops.
Elderkin - old ancient sage, magic, strong.
Harefolk - humanoid bunny, fast, fighting.
Drakescale - lizard dragon, tough, ground.

Plus pool bloodlines, 54, if you want a different race: Elf, Dwarf, Orc, Troll, Goblin, Fairy, Centaur, Werewolf, Demon, Vampire, Unicorn, and the rest, original names, only renamed if copyrighted.

Bloodline decides which classes are available, and base stats.

## Step 2 - Class, Pick 1, Before Bondkin

This is your character class, not your Bondkin job. You pick this first.

## Step 3 - Advanced Class, Unlock Later

Unlock by level and story, after you pick your starter.
Examples:
Warrior to Knight to Paladin to Templar to Grand Templar.
Mage to Black Mage to Archmage to Grand Archmage.
Thief to Assassin to Nightblade to Grand Assassin.
Archer to Sniper to Eaglebow to Grand Archer.
Cleric to White Mage to Bishop to Grand Sage.
Dragoon to Dragon Knight to Grand Dragoon.
Monk to Grand Monk.
Samurai to Grand Samurai.
Summoner to Grand Summoner.
Necromancer to Grand Necromancer.

Advanced Class is your second class, stronger, more moves.

## Step 4 - Bondkin Starter, Pick After Your Class

Now you pick your starter Bondkin, after your character class is set.
Your starter is your first Bondkin, your partner.
Pick 1 of 3 starters, by bloodline, or by type, your call.
Example starters:
- Puffkin starter, magic, fairy.
- Harefolk starter, fighting, fast.
- Drakescale starter, dragon, tough.

Your starter Bondkin has its own job, its own Path, separate from your character class.
Your character class and your Bondkin job work together, they intertwine, but they are not the same.
Your character: Race, Class, Advanced Class.
Your Bondkin: Bloodline, Path, Ascended Path, Twin Path, Twin Ascension.

## Step 5 - Twin Path, Dual Profession

Once you learn it in Advanced Class, you can learn a second Path at once.
Dual profession, two jobs at once.
Example: Warrior + White Mage, Mage + Thief, Archer + Bard.

## Step 6 - Twin Ascension, Dual Dual Advancement

Once you learn Twin Path, you can learn Dual Ascension.
Two advanced jobs at once.
Example: Paladin + Archmage, Nightblade + Grand Assassin, etc.

## Roles, Separate From Class

Roles are for your character, not your Bondkin, from Undawn.
Logger, Miner, Gatherer, Hunter, Chef, Angler, Scavenger, Firearms Master, and more, 10+ roles.
Role XP: chop wood gains logger points, level up as logger to master logger one day.
Character level and XP, gain new abilities, skills, talents.

## Notes

- Character first, Bondkin starter second. That is the order.
- Character level cap 150, Bondkin cap 200.
- Character has Race, Class, Advanced Class, Twin Path, Twin Ascension, plus roles, gear, customization.
- Bondkin has Bloodline, Path, Ascended Path, Twin Path, Twin Ascension, plus evolution, Hollow / Echo variants.
- Abilities cost AP to equip. One ability and one skill per Bondkin, one passive and one active.
- Jobs are learned from equipment. Buy a weapon, learn its skill. Steal to learn.
