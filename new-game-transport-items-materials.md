# FED-13 RPG — Transportation Items and Materials Audit
Audit-only, no file edits. 5 workers, one per source game, all completed.

## Headline gap-fill

Transportation was missing from the items list. The audit found:
- **Kingdom Hearts 2 / Gummi Ship is the only complete transport parts catalogue** — 1 material stock, ~70 named Deco blocks, blueprints, abilities, limit upgrades.
- **Undawn is the only vehicle crafting system** — Mechanic Station, blueprints (generic, no named blueprint found), Fuel, Steel/Screw repair, Highwayman mods.
- **Pokemon Emerald, Trails 1-3, and FFTA have almost no transport crafting materials.** Bikes, HMs, trains, horses, airships, and Chocobos are mostly gifts, story unlocks, dialogue purchases, or scripted rides, not built from parts.
- **No saddle item was found** in FFVII/FFTA. Do not list a saddle as a source item.
- **Bike Voucher does NOT exist in Emerald.** It is FireRed/LeafGreen only.

## 3. Kingdom Hearts 2

### Key system fact
In KH2 the Gummi Ship is the only player-built/owned transport. Built in the Gummi Garage / Gummi Editor, under a Cost Limit. **Gummi Blocks cannot be bought in KH2** — GameFAQs: "No, you can't buy gummi blocks. You increase your stock … by completing missions on the Gummi Routes" (defeating red/gold enemies, Hunters, rank rewards). Opposite of KH1, where Cid sells them.

9 Gummi Routes: Asteroid Sweep (Land of Dragons), Stardust Sweep (Beast's Castle), Phantom Storm (Olympus Coliseum), Splash Island (Atlantica), Floating Island (Port Royal), Ancient Highway (Agrabah), Broken Highway (Halloween Town), Sunlight Storm (Pride Lands), Assault of the Dreadnought (Twilight Town). Each route has 3 missions, unlocked after clearing once. LV1 collect medals / Medal Level, LV2 defeat set number of enemy ships, LV3 high score, player rotates camera manually. Clearing LV1+LV2 unlocks LV3. KH2 Final Mix adds an EX S Mission per mission on S-rank. Rewards by rank S/A/B/C/D/E and as treasures, special/enemy-ship drops; "You must complete a route to save any Treasures that are dropped."

Every block has stats: Cost, HP, Offense, Power (damage multiplier), Speed, Mobility (roll speed).
