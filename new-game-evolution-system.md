# Evolution System - New Game

Rebuilt 2026-11-22 to match the corrected design. Feral Beasts evolve after catch, Stage 1 to 3. Bondkin do NOT evolve species. Hollow and Echo are regional variants, not evolutions.

## Who evolves

- Feral Beasts evolve, after they are caught and trained. A Beast evolves Stage 1 to Stage 3, child to adult to elder, within the same species.
- Wild Feral do not evolve before being caught.
- Bondkin do NOT evolve species. Instead of evolving, a Bondkin advances in 5 stacking tracks inside its own bloodline: Growth Stage, Path to Ascended, Surge Form, Hollow / Echo, Totem Form. A Bondkin can change sub race or clan like a job change. Bloodline, species name root, and Codex number never change.
- Scale: Stage 1 = 0.7, Stage 2 = 0.85, Stage 3 = 1.0, Mega = 1.1. Mega Evolution is a temporary battle form, not a species change.

## How evolution works

- Level: most Beasts evolve by level. Stage 1 to 2 at Lv 16 to 25, Stage 2 to 3 at Lv 32 to 50, depending on family.
- Item: some Beasts evolve by a Growth Item or by a Moon Stone, Sun Stone, or Dusk Stone. Moon, Sun, and Dusk Stones are tied to time: Sun Stone works by day, Moon Stone works by night, Dusk Stone works at dusk.
- No Core Shard evolution: Core Shards are character only, for the Lattice. A Core Shard swap never evolves a Beast and never changes a Beast's type.
- No bond evolution: Bond Lv is for trainer stat passives and joint clan attacks, not for species evolution. There is no Bond Stone evolution item.
- No Hollow or Echo Stones: Hollow Stones and Echo Stones are deleted. Hollow and Echo are regional variants found wild in their regions, never created or triggered by an item. A Hollow Whelp evolves into a Hollow Drake, then a Hollow Dragon. Same for Echo.
- Time / Day / Night: some Beasts evolve only at day, only at night, or only at dusk, by Moon, Sun, or Dusk Stone.
- No evolution: legendaries, Totem Spirits, and some bosses do not evolve. They are Stage 3 only.
- Evolution is a choice: when a Beast is ready to evolve, you can say yes or no. If you say no, it keeps learning moves faster, then you can evolve it later.

## Evolution Items

- Growth Item: general evolution item, found in The Bank, shops, missions.
- Moon Stone, Sun Stone, Dusk Stone: time tied evolution items. Sun Stone works by day, Moon Stone works by night, Dusk Stone works at dusk.
- No Hollow Stone, no Echo Stone, no Bond Stone, no Core Shard evolution. All deleted or character only.

## Notes

- Stage sizes apply at all 4 places creatures are drawn: battle foe, battle back, party thumbs, creature detail, bottom anchored.
- The Codex holds 134 Feral entries. Every entry has a stage, a family, and an evolution method, or is marked no evolution above.
- Hollow and Echo are regional variants, not evolutions, not shiny. A Hollow or Echo variant carries through evolution, it is not an evolution itself.
- No Earth animals in any family. Baby Orc and Baby Elf stay removed as humanoids. Hippogryph stays removed. Harpy stays renamed to Pixel and Siren. Golden Strider is transport, not a catchable Beast, so it has no evolution family.
- Bondkin do not evolve. Beasts evolve. Combo attacks are Bondkin only, fusion is the Beast side.
