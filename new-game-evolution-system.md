# Evolution System - New Game

Bondkin and Feral evolve. Stage 1 smallest, Stage 2 medium, Stage 3 largest.
Scale: Stage 1 = 0.7, Stage 2 = 0.85, Stage 3 = 1.0, Mega = 1.1.
Every Bondkin has a stage. All families are [1, 2, 3], unless it is a legendary, then it is Stage 3 only, no evolution.

## How evolution works

- Level: most evolve by level. Stage 1 to 2 at Lv 16 to 25, Stage 2 to 3 at Lv 32 to 50, depending on family.
- Item: some evolve by a Growth Item, Shard, Stone, or Core Shard swap. Example: swap a Fire Core Shard for an Electric one, creature learns electric moves and can evolve into its electric form.
- Bond: some evolve by bond level. Fight together, bond up, evolve. Bondkin you use a lot evolve faster.
- Time / Day / Night: some evolve only at day, only at night, or only in a specific world.
- Hollow / Echo: Hollow and Echo are NOT evolutions, they are regional variants. A Hollow Whelp evolves into a Hollow Drake, then a Hollow Dragon. Same for Echo.
- No evolution: legendaries, Totem Spirits, and some bosses do not evolve. They are Stage 3 only.
- Evolution is a choice: when a Bondkin is ready to evolve, you can say yes or no. If you say no, it keeps learning moves faster, then you can evolve it later.

## Evolution Items

- Growth Item: general evolution item, found in The Bank, shops, missions.
- Fire Shard, Water Shard, Earth Shard, Air Shard, Dark Shard, Light Shard: evolve into that type.
- Core Shard: swap type, Fire to Electric, etc., can trigger evolution.
- Moon Stone, Sun Stone, Dusk Stone: day / night evolution.
- Bond Stone: bond evolution, need Bond Lv 5+.
- Hollow Stone: evolve into Hollow form, dark region only.
- Echo Stone: evolve into Echo form, light region only.
