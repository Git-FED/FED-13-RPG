# 2 - Bondkin

Bondkin is what a character catches. Bond King is the same thing, your trained partner.
Not the character, not a wild monster. Separate.
A character cannot be the same class as a Bondkin. That does not make sense.
Bondkin jobs and character classes are completely different lists, no overlap.

## Bondkin, In Order

1. Caught By Character
- A character catches a Bondkin.
- Your starter Bondkin is the first one a character catches, after your character class.

2. Bloodline
- Hybrid, Puffkin, Elderkin, Harefolk, Drakescale, plus pool bloodlines.
- Bloodline decides which jobs are available, and base stats.

3. Path, Job, Bondkin Only
- Pick a job, a Path. These are Bondkin only, a character cannot pick these.
- Tank Paths: Warrior, Paladin, Knight, Guardian, Warden.
- Healer Paths: Keeper, White Mage, Cleric, Priest, Druid, Bard.
- Melee Paths: Fighter, Rogue, Assassin, Nightblade, Doomblade, Samurai, Dragoon.
- Caster Paths: Mage, Black Mage, Summoner, Necromancer, Sorcerer, Time Mage.
- Ranged Paths: Archer, Eaglebow, Sniper, Gunner, Hunter.
- Total Paths: 117.

4. Ascended Path
- Advanced job, 30 total, Bondkin only.
- Nightlord, Galerider, Grand Avatar, Shade Saint, Vampyr, and the rest.

5. Twin Path
- Dual profession, learn a second Path at once.

6. Twin Ascension
- Dual dual advancement, two advanced Paths at once.

## Bondkin Only

- Level cap 200.
- Evolution: Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, smallest to largest.
- Hollow and Echo are regional variants, not evolutions.
- Abilities: one ability and one skill, one passive and one active, AP to equip, learned from equipment.
- Roster: 24 in The Bank, deploy 6.
- Bondkin jobs are NOT character classes. No Catcher, no Tamer, no Merchant, no Chef for Bondkin. Those are character only.
