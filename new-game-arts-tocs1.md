# FED-13 RPG - Arts, Trails of Cold Steel 1

Source: Kiseki Wiki, List of orbal arts (Cold Steel), https://kiseki.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_orbal_arts_(Cold_Steel)
Scope: Trails of Cold Steel 1 only. No Lost Arts in CS1, those are CS2+.
Total: 58 Arts. Attack: 33. Healing / Recovery: 12. Support / Passive / Buff / Debuff: 13. Non-attack total: 25.
Cast / Delay are in AT (Action Time ticks), not seconds. Power is the game's internal % / value, not final damage. "Power -" means no damage/healing power value.

Git-FED direction 2026-11-22 ~09:41 EST: Quartz / The Lattice is for the CHARACTER, not Bondkin. Arts will not get to be used as often, and there will be more passive or healing Arts than attack Arts. CS1 itself is still attack-heavy (33 attack vs 25 healing/support), so FED-13 would invert the CS1 ratio, not copy it. That is a design change, not a CS1 fact.

## What could not be verified / flags
- No Arts were invented; every name above appears on the Kiseki Wiki CS1 page.
- Healing Power values (300 / 600 / 1200 / 250 / 550) are the wiki's internal Power column, not a verified in-game HP number for all levels.
- One cross-check: the GameFAQs CS1 guide lists the same EP costs for the Arts sampled (e.g. Adamantine Shield 200 EP, Tear 20 EP, Tearal 120 EP). Not every EP value was re-verified against a second source.
- This file is CS1 only. CS2 and CS3 Arts, Quartz, and Lost Arts are not in this list.
