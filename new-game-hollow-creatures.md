# Hollow Creatures - Regional Variants

Instead of shiny Pokemon. Hollow creatures are regional variants.
Hollow = dark, corrupted, shadow version. Found in dark regions, swamps, ruins, dungeons.
Formerly Heartless. Now Hollow.

## What is a Hollow creature

- Same Bondkin or Feral, different look, different type, different moves.
- Darker colors, glowing eyes, shadow aura.
- Stronger attack, weaker defense, usually Dark type added.
- Found in: Poisonous Swamp, Stinky Area, Ruins, Dungeons, Night zones.
- 1 in 100 chance in the wild, higher in dark regions.
