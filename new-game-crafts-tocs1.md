# FED-13 RPG - Crafts, Trails of Cold Steel 1

Source: Kiseki Wiki, List of crafts (Cold Steel), https://kiseki.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_crafts_(Cold_Steel)
Cross-checked against GameFAQs Zoel CS1 guide (Character Skills section).
Scope: Trails of Cold Steel 1 only. No CS2/CS3 crafts, no True/Fatal/EX variants, those are CS2+.
Totals: 58 regular Crafts + 16 S-Crafts + 2 Valimar Finishers = 76 entries. Regular Crafts: 58. S-Crafts: 16. Valimar Finishers (Brave Points, not CP): 2.

## Gauge, CP, and the IV naming trap
- All regular Crafts and all S-Crafts drain CP (Craft Points).
- CP is built by attacking enemies and by taking damage. GameFAQs: "CP is automatically raised when attacking enemies."
- S-Craft: needs 100+ CP. At 200 CP the S-Craft is much stronger, 50% more damage than at lower CP, per CheatBook/GameFAQs summaries. S-Break: press Triangle at 100+ CP to use the S-Craft immediately, out of turn order, still consuming the CP.
- Kiseki Wiki now labels the cost "IV", that is the same gauge as CP in CS1 English. GameFAQs CS1 lists the identical numbers as CP (e.g. Rean Autumn Leaf Cutter 20 CP, Motivate 30 CP). Do not treat IV as a second gauge.
- Exceptions that do NOT drain CP:
  - Gaius, Wild Rage: costs 30% Max HP, restores 50 CP.
  - Valimar, Spirit: costs 50% EP, heals 50% HP, +50 CP.
  - Valimar Finishers, Destructive Impact / Enlightened Domination: cost 3 BP (Brave Points), not CP.
- Delay is in AT ticks, time until the user's next turn. Power is the game's internal % / value, not final damage. Unbal. = Unbalance chance/effect.

## Character-specific?
Yes, almost entirely. Every Craft below belongs to only that character, with two exceptions:
- Detector is shared: Elliot Craig (Level 9) and Emma Millstein (Initial) both have Detector, same cost/effect (20 CP, Support, single target, Analysis, Balance Down 100%, 4t).
- Flame Impact is a shared name, not a shared Craft: Rean (Level 55, 35 CP, 150% S+, set circle, Burn 50% / Faint 50%) and Valimar (Second Battle, 60 CP, 120%, single target, cannot be guarded/countered, 50% crit) are different entries with different stats.
No other regular Craft or S-Craft is shared between characters.

## Summary by type (regular Crafts only, 58)
- Attack (incl. Magic Attack): 37, most regular Crafts are attacks, often with a status/debuff attached.
- Recovery: 5, Blessed Arrow, Holy Song, Energy Shell, Serene Blessing, plus Resounding Beat is Support with HP Regen (counted under Support below).
- Support: 16, Motivate, Heavenly Gift, Resounding Beat, Detector (x2 characters, 1 name), Radiant Wings, Burst Drive, Crescent Shell, Noble Command, Ultimate Barrier, Wild Rage, Lightning Charge, Dragon Boost, Qinggong, Morning Moon, Spirit, Wild Card.
- S-Crafts: 16, all Attack (Alisa's two are Magic Attack; Elliot's and Emma's are Magic Attack). No healing/support S-Craft in CS1.
Note on the count: Detector appears twice (Elliot + Emma) in the 58 regular entries, so there are 57 distinct regular Craft names, 58 character-Craft entries.

## What could NOT be verified / flags
1. Sara, Thundering Roar, Seal % conflict on the Kiseki page itself: the type line says "Seal (90%)", the detail line says "Seal (30% chance, 4t)". I used 30% in the detail and flagged the 90% here; do not present either as settled without a second source check.
2. Kiseki "IV" vs GameFAQs "CP": the Kiseki page lists costs as IV; GameFAQs CS1 lists the same numbers as CP. I treated them as the same gauge. If FED-13 renames the gauge, pick one name and use it consistently.
3. 200 CP S-Craft bonus: verified only from secondary guides (CheatBook/GameFAQs summaries: 50% more damage at 200 CP). I did not verify the exact 50% figure against the Kiseki craft page, which lists S-Crafts only as "100+ CP".
4. CP gain rate: "built by attacking and taking damage" is verified in general terms; exact CP gain per hit / per damage taken was not verified and is not in this list.
5. No Crafts were invented. Every name above appears on the Kiseki Wiki CS1 craft list as read 2026-11-22. This task was CS1 only, CS2/CS3 Crafts, True/Fatal variants, and Chain/Combo Crafts are not in this list.
