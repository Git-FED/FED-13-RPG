# 3 - Monsters

Monsters are Feral, wild monsters a character catches. Not the character, not Bondkin. Separate.
A monster becomes a Bondkin after a character catches and trains it.

## Monsters, Feral

- Wild, in Feral wilds, routes, dungeons, forests.
- No random encounters, enemies visible on field.
- A character catches a monster.
- After catch and train, a monster becomes a Bondkin.
- 24 roster, 6 deployed.
- No basic Earth animals. No wolves, bears, cats, lions, horses, rats, frogs, snakes, spiders, etc. Fantasy only.

## Monsters Only

- Evolution: Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, see evolution system file.
- Hollow and Echo are regional variants, not shiny.
- Monsters have no class. Monsters do not have character classes, and monsters do not have Bondkin jobs, until a character catches and trains them, then they become Bondkin and get a Bondkin job.
