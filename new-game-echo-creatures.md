# Echo Creatures - Regional Variants

Instead of shiny Pokemon. Echo creatures are regional variants.
Echo = ghostly, pale, faded version. Found in light regions, ruins, sky zones, disconnected worlds.
Formerly Nobodies. Now Echo.

## What is an Echo creature

- Same Bondkin or Feral, different look, different type, different moves.
- Pale white, silver, translucent, ghostly glow.
- Stronger defense and speed, weaker attack, usually Ghost or Fairy type added.
- Found in: Disconnected Worlds, Sky zones, Ruins, Light zones, Snow zones.
- 1 in 100 chance in the wild, higher in light regions.
