# FED-13 RPG - README

FED for Git-FED, 13 for The Thirteen.
No design, no build until Git-FED finishes picking features.
Assets first: create ALL assets before building anything.
4 directional sprites for every thing, front, back, left, right, plus battle front and back.
Graphics: Level 3, 256 by 256, HD 2D, a lot more fur and shading.
100% own assets from scratch, no copyrighted assets, so everything can be sold.
Only rename copyrighted IP. Generic names stay.
Source games: Pokemon Emerald Hacked ROMs, Kingdom Hearts 2, Trails of Cold Steel 3, Undawn, Final Fantasy Tactics Advance.
Slack: #video-game. GitHub: https://github.com/Git-FED/FED-13-RPG

## Main Character

You, the player. Not a Bondkin, not a monster. Cap 150.
No race choice for the main character.
Class, character only. Flow: Class, Advanced Class, Twin Path, Twin Ascension.
Twin Path is Dual Profession. Twin Ascension is Dual Dual Advancement.
Opening choice: Sword, Shield, Staff.
World form changes: only for the main character.
Crafts cost CP. CP built by attacking and taking damage.
S-Crafts: super moves, 100+ CP, stronger at 200+ CP. S-Break: press anytime at 100+ CP.
Character skills, from Undawn, not classes, not Bondkin jobs: Logger, Miner, Gatherer, Hunter, Chef, Angler, Scavenger, Firearms Master, etc.
Role XP: chop wood gains logger points, level up as logger to master logger one day.

## Bondkin

What you catch. Your trained partner. First Bondkin is your starter, rest are partners.
Cap 200. Not you, not a wild monster anymore once you catch it.
5 core bloodlines, race determines jobs and base stats:
Hybrid, Puffkin, Elderkin, Harefolk, Drakescale.
Hybrid is human-like, and Hybrid is not catchable. Humans are not to be caught.

Most balanced, widest jobs, 11 total.

### Drakescale - Bangaa

Fierce, lizard-like warriors, high physical attack and defense. 7 unique jobs, Warrior, Dragoon, Gladiator, no other race can access.
Calling a Bangaa a lizard is a severe insult in-universe. They are warm-blooded mammals, live birth, not eggs.
Lifespan 1.5 to 2 times Hybrid, up to 120 to 150 years. Aging shows as long braided facial hair, dulled, scarred scales, not wrinkles.
No king, no unified monarch. Led by clan caste roles, Ruga priests, lawmakers, and honorific prefixes like Ba'.
FED-13 sub-races, original names, same roles:
Solwrights - Yellow-gold scales, shorter ears, gentle scholars, priests, monks, instructors, lawmakers. Was Ruga.
Bronzeguard - Bronze scales, heavy athletic build, frontline warriors, enforce Drakescale law alongside Solwrights. Was Faas, Faas means soldier, strong sense of justice.
Emberfolk - Copper scales, relaxed, easygoing, integrate smoothly into Hybrid-dominated cities. Was Bista.
Ashenmoor - Ash-grey scales, laid-back, live alongside Hybrids in urban trade hubs. Was Sanga.
Stonehold - Mountain stronghold town. Was Sprohm.
Leader prefix: Dru' / Vor' in FED-13, e.g. Dru'Gamnan style, rank, clan standing, not age alone. Was Ba' / Va'.
Young Bangaa gang leader Mocedad, Spanish for youth, adolescence, in FFTA2, becomes Youngblood leader called Mocen in FED-13.
Young: Drakeling, Child, Offspring. Do NOT use Hatchling or Whelp as the official young title, FFTA lore rejects them as an insult, even though Drakescale look reptilian. In FED-13 Drakescale are dragons, so Drakeling is the own FED-13 word.
Elder: Elder, Old Drakescale. Leader: Dra' prefix, Chief, Ruga Council / High Priest, Bishop, Templar for Solwright leaders.

### Elderkin - Nu Mou

Rarest sentient species in Ivalice. Long-lived, 3x Hume lifespan in wider lore.
No biological sub-races. Organised by academic, religious, and alchemical orders, not bloodline clans.

Elder: Elder, Master Scholar, High Elder, Master Acolyte. Leader: High Scholar, Master Alchemist, Master Sage.

### Harefolk - Viera

All-female in FFTA presentation, rabbit-eared forest race, top speed and evasion. 8 jobs, Fencer, Sniper, Elementalist, Summoner, Assassin.
Eight in ten Viera born are women in FFXIV lore. Males are taken by Wood-warders to train in the deep forest.
FED-13 sub-races, original names:

Snowpine - Fair-skinned, pale silver-white hair, high-altitude mountain forests, isolated matriarchal villages, guarded by unseen male Wood-warders. Was Veena, Skatay Range.
Palebranch Exiles - Pale-skinned, short-eared, blonde hair, half-Harefolk, rare, longer lifespan, shunned as outcasts by traditional forest clans.
Cultural divide: Wood-Dwellers vs City-Dwellers. Wood-Dwellers obey The Wood Law, sacred law forbidding contact with outside world, was The Green Word. City-Dwellers leave, lose the voice of the wood, discard village surnames, permanently exiled.
Woodwarden, male protectors, was Wood-warder.
Young: Kit. Kit stays, this is the one official species-specific young term in FFTA / FFXIV lore, for rabbit-like Viera. Kits are physically indistinguishable by gender until about age 13.
Elder: Elder, Matriarch. Leader: Matriarch, Woodwarden for males.

## Bondkin Advancement - Change Sub Race or Clan, Not Evolution

No evolution for Bondkin. Instead of evolving, a Bondkin can change sub race or clan.
Bloodline never changes, species never changes, Codex 001 to 005 never changes.
Changing sub race or clan changes stats, jobs available, and appearance, like a job change.
Children versions exist for species, so Feral species do technically evolve, child to adult to elder, within the same species.

### Growth Stage, Feral Only

Feral monsters evolve Stage 1 to 3, only after catch, as a Bondkin. Wild Feral do not evolve before being caught.
Stage 1 = 0.7, Stage 2 = 0.85, Stage 3 = 1.0.
Evolution is a choice, say no and it keeps learning moves faster, evolve later.
Methods: Level, Item, Growth Item, Shard, Core Shard swap, Bond Lv 5+, Time day / night, Moon / Sun / Dusk Stone.
Hollow and Echo are regional variants, not evolutions, not shiny. Hollow Stone and Echo Stone are deleted.
Legendaries and Totem Spirits are Stage 3 only, no evolution.
5 core Bondkin grow by sub race, clan change, Path change, Surge Form, Hollow, Echo, and Totem Form, not by species evolution.

### Path to Ascended Path - FFTA Job Advancement

Path = FFTA primary job. Ascended Path = FFTA expert, secondary job.
Unlock exactly like FFTA: master 1 to 5 Action Abilities in the base Path, learned from weapons, accessories with AP, then the Ascended Path unlocks. No level-only unlock.
Change job any time outside battle, no penalty, no level loss. Learned abilities stay learned after changing jobs.
FFTA has 42 race-job slots, 34 unique jobs.
Human 11, Viera 8, Nu Mou 8, Bangaa 7, Moogle 8.

### Surge Forms and Form Skills

All of Sora's learnable skills are form skills. When a Bondkin changes its form, it unlocks a new set of skills, also unlockable per form level.
Surge Forms, in Git-FED's final order:
1. Gale Form - Quick Run / Air Slide
2. Aegis Form - Dodge Roll
3. Zephyr Form - Glide
4. Tempest Form - Aerial Dodge
5. Zenith Form - High Jump
Form levels 1 to 7, per Bondkin. Lv3 = skill Lv1, Lv5 = skill Lv2, Lv7 = skill Lv3 / MAX.
Surge Gauge fills with drive orbs, taking damage, attacks. Transforming fully refills HP and MP.
Temporary: enter form in battle, leave form after battle. Bloodline, species, job do not change.
Old form names are dead, do not use: Valor, Wisdom, Master, Final, Limit, Might, Bastion. Aether is liked but unplaced, no form yet.

### Totem Spirits - Special Attack, Mega, Z-Move

Totem Spirits: own version of Totema, keep all. Each race, bloodline, gets a Totem Spirit, a guardian.
Totem Spirit attacks are special attacks, a move a Bondkin can use while Mega evolved, or as a Z-move if Mega is not used.
Mega Evolution is a temporary battle form for the Totem move. This overrides the old No Mega line.
Summoner classes sound wild in a Pokemon-like game, but Totem is a guardian move, not a full summon class.
FFTA Totema, 5, one per race, summoned at 10 Judge Points, JP: Famfrit, Moogle, Ultima, Nu Mou, Adrammelech, Bangaa, Exodus, Viera, Mateus, Human. Do NOT reuse those FF names in FED-13, they need original FED-13 names. Totem Spirits are Stage 3 only, summoned at 10 Path Points, formerly 10 JP.
Totem Form proposal: when a Bondkin summons its bloodline Totem Spirit, it gains a temporary Totem Form for that battle, larger, Totem-marked, Totem abilities only.
Decisions open: Mega as default, or Z-move as default, and name the 5 Totem Spirits.

### Beastmaster and Morpher, Soul System

Beastmaster: Nu Mou, Elderkin only, primary job, no requirement. Control command, 200 AP per type, learned from instruments, bells, flutes, harps.
Control types, 13: Goblin, Flan, Bomb, Dragon, Lamia, Bug, Grudgeknife, Panther, Foulbloom, Floateye, Undead, Fairy, Rockbeast.
Control is not transform, not capture. It controls an enemy monster's actions for a turn.
Beastmaster is useful for battles with a lot of monsters.
Morpher: Nu Mou, Elderkin only, secondary job, requires 5x Beastmaster A-Abilities. Only true form-change in FFTA.
Morpher, Soul system, agreed: Hunter captures a Feral, it goes to The Bank, it drops a Soul, a Morpher-type Bondkin equips the Soul and morphs into that monster type in battle, with power tied to Bank feeding.
Hunter: Human, Hybrid only, 2x Archer A-Abilities, Capture ability, Ranger Bow. Captures a monster in battle, injured monster only, max about 24% chance, undead, rockbeast, fairy, Grudgeknife cannot be captured, cannot be the last enemy.
Morph forms, 9 types: Goblin, Flan, Bomb, Dragon, Lamia, Bug, Panther, Foulbloom, Floateye. Morph learned per type, 200 AP each. Power depends on stats of monsters of that type in The Bank, feeding them items raises their stats.
Morph Form proposal: a 6th, non-Surge form change. A Bondkin that has mastered Morpher, Elderkin only per FFTA, can temporarily morph into a captured Feral's form in battle, using that Feral's moveset, for one battle. It does not become that Feral.
Decision open: Morph Form Elderkin Morpher only, or any Bondkin that masters Morpher?

### Hollow and Echo Variants

Hollow: dark, corrupted, shadow, Dark type added, stronger attack, weaker defense. Found in dark regions.
Echo: ghostly, pale, faded, Ghost type added, stronger defense and speed, weaker attack. Found in light regions.
Variant carries through Growth Stage, Path change, and Surge Form. A Hollow Puffkin grows to Elder Hollow Puffkin, changes Path to Puffkin Knight, and enters Gale Form as a Hollow Gale Form.
Hollow, Echo are not evolutions, not shiny, and there is no Hollow Stone, Echo Stone.
New variant proposal, Git-FED to approve: Ascended variant, after mastering an Ascended Path, permanent visual variant, gold markings, Totem sigil, no type change. Shard variant, swapping a Core Shard changes affinity and appearance tint, Fire to Electric, etc., already in the Lattice rule.

## Battle, World, Story, Transport, Systems
