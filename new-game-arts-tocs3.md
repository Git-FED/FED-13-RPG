# FED-13 RPG - Arts, Trails of Cold Steel 3

Source: Kiseki Wiki, List of orbal arts (Cold Steel III), https://kiseki.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_orbal_arts_(Cold_Steel_III)
Audit-only, no files edited. No Arts invented.
Cast = time until the Art fires, in AT ticks. Delay = time until the caster's next turn after casting, in AT ticks.
Type is the Wiki's own classification: Attack / Recovery / Support. Power is the Wiki's % value (damage/heal strength). "-" means the Wiki lists no power (pure buff/cure).
All regular Arts have 100% Break multiplier; an Art of an element the enemy is weak to does +50% final Break damage.
Lost Arts are NOT in CS3 on this page. CS2 had 5 Lost Arts (Sol Eruption, Artemis' Tears, Grail Burst, Tempestuous Roar, Lost Eden). CS3's page has no Lost Arts section at all. Do not count them as CS3 Arts.

Totals, verified count:
- Regular Orbal Arts: 51 total
  - Offensive (Attack): 25
  - Recovery + Support (healing / passive / buff / debuff / cure): 26
  - So in CS3 itself, healing/support (26) already slightly outnumber attack (25), before any FED-13 rebalancing toward Git-FED's "more passive or healing Arts than attack Arts" rule.
- EX Arts (mech battles only, no cast time): 22 total
  - Attack: 9 (7 generic elemental + Chivalry Runes + Thundering Roar)
  - Support / Recovery: 13
- Grand total if EX Arts are counted: 73. Regular only: 51.

## NEW IN CS3 vs CS1 / CS2

Compared every CS3 name against the full CS2 list on Kiseki Wiki (List of orbal arts (Cold Steel II)).

NEW offensive Arts in CS3 (17 of 25 offensive Arts are new names vs CS2):
- Earth: Ivy Nail, Megalith Fall, Gravion Hammer (3)
- Water: Crystal Edge, Blue Ascension, Diamond Nova (3)
- Fire: Venom Flame, Flare Butterfly, Zeruel Cannon (3)
- Wind: Nemesis Arrow, Aerial Dust, Ixion Volt (3)
- Time: Calvary Edge, Lost Genesis (2)
- Space: Seventh Caliber (1)
- Mirage: Galion Fort, Albion Wolf (2)

Kept from CS2 (8 offensive names, rebalanced, costs, cast, delay, power, and secondary effects all changed in CS3): Needle Shoot, Aqua Bleed, Fire Bolt, Air Strike, Soul Blur, Golden Sphere, Cross Crusade, Silver Thorn.

NEW support/recovery Arts in CS3 vs CS2: NONE by name. All 26 support/recovery names in CS3 already existed in CS2 (Earth Pulse, Crest, La Crest, Adamantine Shield, Tear, Teara, Tearal, Curia, Thelas, Athelas, Impassion, Forte, La Forte, Heat Up, Breath, Holy Breath, Recuria, Chrono Drive, Chrono Break, Chrono Burst, Fortuna, Shining, Seraphic Ring, Analyze, Saintly Force, Crescent Mirror). Their costs, cast/delay, areas, and some effects were rebalanced in CS3. E.g. Saintly Force was STR/DEF/SPD in CS2, STR/ATS in CS3; Crescent Mirror was Magic Reflect + ATS in CS2, Magic Reflect + DEF/ADF in CS3; Seraphic Ring was full heal + HP Regen in CS2, full heal only in CS3.

Removed / not in CS3 (present in CS2, absent in CS3): Earth Lance, Grand Press, Yggdrasil, Ancient Glyph; Frost Edge, Hydro Cannon, Crystal Flood, Maelstrom; Heatwave, Flame Tongue, Volcanic Rain, Flare Bomb, Purgatorial Flame; Spark Arrow, Aerial, Judgment Bolt, Ragna Vortex; Demonic Scythe, Grim Butterfly, Shadow Blade; Dark Matter, Altair Cannon; Luminous Ray, Phantom Phobia, Claiomh Solarion; plus all 5 CS2 Lost Arts.

FLAGGED, CS1 comparison NOT fully verified: I did not do a line-by-line CS1 list check in this pass. The CS2 comparison above is verified. CS1's list is a subset of the CS2-era set for most elements, so the "new in CS3" names above are almost certainly also new vs CS1, but that specific CS1-vs-CS3 diff is unverified, do not present it as checked without a CS1 pass.

Name-variant note (not separate Arts): Gematsu's pre-release CS3 article calls Seventh Caliber "Sevens Calibur", Albion Wolf "Alvion Wolf", and Zeruel Cannon "Zeruel Canon". Those are the same Arts under pre-localisation spellings.

## Relevance to FED-13
Per Git-FED 2026-11-22 ~09:41 EST: Quartz / The Lattice is for the character, not Bondkin. Arts will be used less often, and there will be more passive or healing Arts than attack Arts.
CS3's own split (26 healing/support vs 25 attack, regular Arts) already leans that way slightly. The healing core is Water (Tear line + Curia + Thelas/Athelas revive), plus Wind Breath/Holy Breath, Earth Pulse, and Seraphic Ring as the full-party revive.
The passive/buff core is: Crest/La Crest (DEF), Forte/La Forte (STR), Fortuna (ATS/ADF), Chrono Drive (SPD/MOV), Saintly Force (STR/ATS + cure stat-down), Crescent Mirror (Art Reflect + DEF/ADF), Adamantine Shield (physical immunity), Shining (Insight), Analyze (enemy data).
The debuff/status core is almost entirely on attack Arts (Poison, Petrify, Mute, Freeze, Burn, Blind, Seal, Nightmare, Confuse, stat-downs, AT Delay, Deathblow), plus Chrono Break (SPD down + AT Delay) as the only pure support debuff.
