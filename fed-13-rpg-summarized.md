# FED-13 RPG - Summarized 🎮

## What it is ✨
A new RPG built from 5 games: Pokemon Emerald hacked ROMs, Kingdom Hearts 2, Trails of Cold Steel 3, Undawn, Final Fantasy Tactics Advance. You pick what you like and hate from each. No design, no build yet, assets first when design is approved.

## Name 🏷️
FED-13 RPG. FED for Git-FED, 13 for The Thirteen.

## Basic structure 🧑🤝🐺
Mankind, Bondkin, Beast. Nothing else.

🧑 Mankind: you, the trainer, race Polar or Solar, cap 150.

❄️ Polar: the palest skin tones, from very pale European to light Asian and light Mediterranean / Spanish.
☀️ Solar: the deepest skin tones, from South Asian / Indian through darker Asian, Pacific Islander / Hawaiian, Black, and dark African.

🤝 Bondkin: your partner creature, starter, caught and trained, cap 200.

5 bloodlines of Bondkin 🩸:

Hybrid, Puffkin, Elderkin, Harefolk, Drakescale. Race decides jobs and stats.

Bondkin do not evolve species. They advance in 5 tracks inside their own bloodline, and can change sub race / clan, like a job change.
Bondkin have full children 👶, high rarity possible, triple the time of a Beast egg.
Bondkin have a daycare 🍼, no Monster Bank.

🐺 Beast: Feral, wild, catchable later. A Beast becomes a Bondkin after catch and train.

Beasts are in The Bank 🏦. Beasts breed eggs 🥚. Beasts can fuse, Pokemon Infinite Fusion style, via breeding, the daycare, or breeding items. Mankind and Bondkin cannot fuse.

## Battle ⚔️
1v1: normal Pokemon style.
2v2 to 6v6: FFTA small grid turn based team battle, larger map for more creatures.
Roster 24, deploy 6. 2.5D isometric, DS style, no random encounters.
Combo attacks are the most important thing, Bondkin only, Beasts get fusion instead. Link Attacks, combo abilities spending Path Points, joint clan attacks costing 10 Judge Points. Progression: 13 Trial Battles, one member of The Thirteen per world, beat the member to unlock new worlds, Elite Four and Hall of Fame after all 13.

## Forms 🔄
Surge Forms: 💨 Gale Form, 🛡️ Aegis Form, 🍃 Zephyr Form, 🌪️ Tempest Form, ⭐ Zenith Form. Levels 1-7. Mega is a temporary battle form for the Totem move, Z-move is backup.

🌑 Hollow: dark, Dark type, stronger attack. 👻 Echo: ghostly, Ghost type, stronger defense and speed. Regional variants, not evolutions.

## Crafts 🛠️
Per race, exclusive, 30 total, 6 per race. CP gauge, regular 20-80 CP, S-Crafts 100+ CP, stronger at 200 CP, all CP lost on K.O. True Lv 46, EX Lv 81, 2 Lv 114.

## Quartz / Lattice 💎
Quartz / Core Shard is character only, not Bondkin. Arts are Bondkin jobs, added to the FFTA job list. Skills are learned via items, attached to weapons like FFTA. U-Material: character quartz and weapons only. EP Charge: CS3 set.

## Items 🎒
Lean list, 104 entries, 98 core items. Character, class, role material, creature, monster piles, separate.

## World 🗺️
Realm Map plus 12 disconnected worlds, 13 worlds, one member of The Thirteen per world, 65 towns, 5 per world. Worlds are reached by Sky Galleon, the Sky Skiff is fast travel within a world, plus Golden Strider, rollerblades, skateboard, bike, boat, kayak, wagon and horse. Professions: Pilot, Sailor, Rider, Engineer, Cook, Gatherer, you cannot use any transportation without the proper profession, Pilot license from a flight school. Every disconnected world grants a new weapon themed to that world, see new-game.md Decisions section for the 12 weapons. Hollow region is Duskhollow, Echo region is Lumenvale. The Thirteen are the hidden manipulators. No Earth animals. No Disney, keep themes only.

## Story / visuals 🎬
Dialogue boxes with portraits, not text heavy. 20-30 cutscenes, 5-10 voiced. Level 3, 256x256, HD 2D, 4 directional sprites. App images: 2D, not realistic, not cute.
Grimoire 📖: an AI assisted in-game wiki. Four Grimoires: Muse, Grok, the Wisp, the Chronicler. Switch at any Puffkin shop for 500 Crests, contacts and notes carry over. Which guide you get depends on the opening choice: Sword gets a fairy, magic gets a mystical orb, and the in-between choice gets the Grimoire. The mystical orb and the fairy do the same job as the Grimoire: the wiki and the contacts. The Grimoire is FED-13's PokeNav, the in-game device like the PokeNav and other Pokemon devices. It is also a contact book: characters write each other's names in each other's Grimoires, like the PokeNav Match Call, so they can contact each other for a rematch. Any question the character has can be solved by their Grimoire. It looks like a mystical conversation, but it is just a generic AI chat loaded with answers about the game.
