# Fed MMORPG - Sora Skills, Kingdom Hearts

Source: KH Wiki, Kingdom Hearts Wiki, KH guides. Workers searched the web, 2026-11-21.
Note: There is no standalone ability literally called Fly in KH1/KH2. Glide / Superglide is the fly.
Quick Run = Air Slide, same technique, called Quick Run in KH2, Air Slide elsewhere.

## Kingdom Hearts 1
- Dodge Roll: roll to evade, defeat Guard Armor.
- High Jump: jump higher, chest near Geppetto in Monstro.
- Mermaid Kick: swim faster in Atlantica, defeat Ursula.
- Glide: ride air currents in midair, hold to stay aloft, seal the Keyhole in Neverland.
- Superglide: fly at high speed in midair, defeat Chernabog.
- Sliding Dash: dash toward an enemy and attack, KH1 Final Mix.

## Kingdom Hearts 2, Growth Abilities
Learned by leveling Drive Forms, not by character level. Form Lv3 = Lv1, Lv5 = Lv2, Lv7 = Lv3, Form MAX at Lv7.
- High Jump, Valor Form: jump higher, up to 320% at max.
- Quick Run / Air Slide, Wisdom Form: rapid ground dash, hold for distance, up to 370% run speed at max.
- Aerial Dodge, Master Form: second jump in midair, jump height 150%, longer midair combos.
- Glide, Final Form: ride the wind in midair, faster than running, speed and air time increase with level.
- Dodge Roll, Limit Form, KH2 Final Mix only: short forward roll, invulnerable while rolling.
- Sliding Dash: instantly closes in on and attacks a far target, clear the Village Cave, Land of Dragons.
- No Superglide in KH2, max Glide Lv3 is as fast as KH1 Superglide.

## Kingdom Hearts 3
- High Jump: leap high, defeat Angelic Amber.
- Doubleflight: press jump in midair to jump a second time, defeat the Frost Serpents, Arendelle.
- Dodge Roll: ground roll, default.
- Air Slide: air dash, stackable, default.
- Aerial Dodge: spin in midair to evade, combat only, default.
- Glide: hold jump in midair to glide at high speed, defeat the Enemy Swarm, Keyblade Graveyard.
- Flowmotion: Wall Kick, Pole Spin, Pole Swing, Superjump, Superslide, Rail Slide, Kick Dive, Buzz Saw, Blow-Off, Wheel Rush, Sliding Dive, Shock Dive.

## Kingdom Hearts 3D: Dream Drop Distance, Sora
