The Codex

Wild Ferals are encountered in routes, ruins and dungeons. Catch one, train it, and it becomes a Bondkin with a path of its own.

#001 / BondkinPuffkin

Floating shopkeeper. Carries snacks. Knows the best deals.

#002 / BondkinElderkin

Tiny sage wizard with a huge opinion about your Shards.

#003 / BondkinHarefolk

Fast, fierce and suspiciously good at jumping over chores.

#004 / BondkinDrakescale

Loyal dragon-blooded companion. Excellent at dramatic entrances.

#011 / FeralDragon

Mountain lair resident. Fire breath. Top-tier paperwork.

#015 / FeralWyvern

Two-legged sky hunter with a fast dive and a bad attitude.

#019 / FeralElemental

Living fire, water, earth or air. Never the same commute twice.

#057 / FeralDoppleganger

Shapeshifter. May already be reading this over your shoulder.

#020 / FeralSkeleton

Undead bone, endless stamina, zero need for a chair.

#025 / FeralLich

Death magic specialist with an extremely organized crypt.

#061 / FeralWisp

Floating light that drains mana and steals the spotlight.

#043 / FeralSea Serpent

Huge, aquatic, and not interested in your tiny boat.
