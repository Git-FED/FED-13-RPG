# FED-13 RPG - Arts, Trails of Cold Steel 2

Source (primary): Kiseki Wiki, List of orbal arts (Cold Steel II), https://kiseki.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_orbal_arts_(Cold_Steel_II)
Comparison source CS1 vs CS2: Kiseki Wiki, List of orbal arts (Cold Steel), https://kiseki.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_orbal_arts_(Cold_Steel)
Definitions: Attack = deals damage, may have extra effects. Recovery = restores HP or status. Support = grants bonuses to allies or inflicts penalties on enemies. Cast = time until the art is used, in AT. Delay = time until the user gets their next turn after casting, in AT. Power is an internal value, useful only for relative comparison.

Totals CS2:
- Offensive (regular): 33
- Recovery / Support (regular): 26 (Recovery-tagged: 11, Support-tagged: 15)
- Regular total: 59
- Lost Arts: 5
- EX Arts (Divine Knight / Valimar only): 15
- Grand total listed: 79
Non-attack Arts are 26 of 59 regular Arts (44%), healing alone is 11, before Lost / EX Arts.

## 3. Lost Arts, 5, CS2 only

Lost Arts are only available by equipping special quartz obtained from defeating strong Cryptids. They use 100% of the caster's EP, the caster must have full EP to cast, each can only be cast once per battle, and damage Lost Arts ignore elemental efficacy. EP-cost-reducing quartz also reduces the amount used, per the Kiseki page.
- Sol Eruption, The Sun, 100% EP, Cast 30 AT, Delay 30 AT, Power 600%, Attack, all targets, DEF/SPD -50%, 5 turns, Burn 200%, 3 turns, removes positive effects
- Artemis' Tears, The Moon, 100% EP, Cast 30 AT, Delay 30 AT, Recovery, all targets, recovers 200% HP, overheals to twice max HP
- Grail Burst, The Grail, 100% EP, Cast 0 AT, Delay 0 AT, Support, all targets, Accelerate, targets act immediately, user acts twice in succession
- Tempestuous Roar, The Dragon, 100% EP, Cast 30 AT, Delay 30 AT, Support, all targets, restores 100 CP, CP Regen 30 CP/turn, 3 turns
- Lost Eden, The Holy, 100% EP, Cast 30 AT, Delay 30 AT, Power 600%, Attack, all targets, STR/ATS -50%, 5 turns, Confuse 200%, 3 turns, removes positive effects

## 5. New in CS2 vs CS1

No new regular offensive Arts by name. All 33 offensive Arts in CS2 have the same names as in CS1. What changed is numbers, not names: CS2 generally has lower EP costs and much faster Cast / Delay.
- Needle Shoot: CS1 20 EP, 5 AT cast, 20 AT delay, 180% - CS2 30 EP, 5 AT cast, 18 AT delay, 150%
- Yggdrasil: CS1 240 EP, 15 AT cast, 25 AT delay, 330% - CS2 180 EP, 10 AT cast, 20 AT delay, 250%
- Purgatorial Flame: CS1 400 EP, 25 AT cast, 30 AT delay - CS2 450 EP, 12 AT cast, 24 AT delay
- Chrono Burst: CS1 200 EP - CS2 400 EP, still 0 AT cast / 0 AT delay

One apparent new regular Art by name: Curia (Water, 20 EP, 3 AT cast, 18 AT delay, cures status). It is in the CS2 Water support list and I did not find it in the CS1 Kiseki list, which lists only Tear, Teara, Tearal, Thelas, Athelas for Water support. Treat this as name-comparison only, not independently confirmed. GameFAQs CS1 guides do list Curia, so it may be a Kiseki CS1 page omission rather than a true CS2 addition. Do not present it as settled without a second source check.

One changed Art, not a new name: Saintly Force (Mirage). CS1: STR/DEF +25%, 4 turns, cures Stat Down. CS2: STR/DEF/SPD +25%, 5 turns, cures Stat Down.

New systems / categories in CS2, not in the CS1 Kiseki list:
- Lost Arts: all 5 are new, Sol Eruption, Artemis' Tears, Grail Burst, Tempestuous Roar, Lost Eden. No Lost Arts section exists on the CS1 page.
- EX Arts: all 15 are new as a category, Divine Knight partner Arts. No EX Arts section exists on the CS1 page.

Healing / passive vs attack, CS2 regular Arts:
- Attack: 33
- Recovery: 11, Earth Pulse, Tear, Teara, Tearal, Thelas, Athelas, Curia, Breath, Holy Breath, Recuria, Seraphic Ring
- Support / passive / buff / debuff: 15, Crest, La Crest, Adamantine Shield, Impassion, Heat Up, Forte, La Forte, Chrono Drive, Chrono Break, Chrono Burst, Fortuna, Shining, Analyze, Saintly Force, Crescent Mirror
- Non-attack regular: 26 / 59. Attack regular: 33 / 59.
With Lost + EX included, attack-type rises to 41 (33 + Sol Eruption + Lost Eden + 6 attack EX Arts), healing/support rises to 38 (26 + Artemis' Tears + Grail Burst + Tempestuous Roar + 9 support EX Arts).

## 6. What could NOT be verified / flags
1. Resounding Beat vs Resonant Beat: The Kiseki CS2 EX Arts table lists both, both for Elliot, both 200 EP / 30 AT delay, HP Regen 10% vs 15%. This looks like a duplicate / upgrade pair, or a wiki error. I did not verify which one is correct, or whether one is CS1 and one is CS2. Do not use both without a second check.
2. Curia as CS2-new: see above, name-comparison only, contradicted by GameFAQs CS1 guides listing Curia. Unresolved.
3. La Crest area: Kiseki CS2 says circle around target, 8 radius. GameFAQs / Neoseeker CS2 tables say Area (M), and CS1 Kiseki says 6 radius. I used Kiseki CS2, but the area size is inconsistent across sources.
4. Earth Pulse duration / strength: Kiseki CS2 says HP Regen 20% max HP/turn, 5 turns. GameFAQs says HP Regen, 4 turns, no %. I used Kiseki CS2.
5. Seraphic Ring regen: Kiseki CS2 says 20% max HP/turn, 3 turns. CS1 Kiseki says 30%, 4 turns. That CS2 weakening is as listed, not a typo.
6. Lost Art quartz / Cryptid source names: I verified the 5 Lost Art names, costs, and effects from the Kiseki page, but I did not verify the individual Cryptid / quartz drop for each one. Do not cite a specific Cryptid per Lost Art from this report.
7. Power grades: GameFAQs / Neoseeker use letter grades (B, A, S, SS, SSS, SSSS) and different Cast Time numbers (e.g. 500, 1000, 1200) from a different system. I used Kiseki AT / % values throughout and did not reconcile the two systems.
8. No Arts were invented. Every name above appears on the Kiseki CS2 page as read 2026-11-22.
