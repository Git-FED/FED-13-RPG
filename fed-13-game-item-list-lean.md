# FED-13 RPG - Game Item List, Lean

One lean item list for FED-13 RPG. This replaces the 83,482-byte combined list (710 lines, about 450 claimed items). Duplicates, category-only entries counted as items, and source catalogues pasted into the main list are cut. Source catalogues are kept in Appendices A-E, condensed, not re-listed in full in the main list.

## Rules this list follows

- Character is the trainer, cap 150. Creatures are Bondkin, cap 200. Monsters are Pokemon-like Feral. Creatures and monsters get used like Pokemon in battle, but are treated different outside of battle.
- Single battles are normal Pokemon style battles. Anything after that, 2v2 and up to 6v6, is grid turn based team style battle.
- Items structure: character items, class items, and role material are separate categories. Character tools are separate from creature items, categorized by the FFTA item listing. Monsters do not have weapons or equipment, instead they have monster related items.
- The Lattice, with Shards and Core Shards, is for the CHARACTER, not Bondkin. A Core Shard does NOT determine Bondkin affinity. That earlier source line is superseded by Git-FED's rule, 2026-11-22.
- U-Material is for only what it applies to: character quartz upgrades (to Rare / Super Rare) and character weapon upgrades. It does not upgrade Bondkin gear. What U-Material upgrades in Trails is quartz upgrades and weapon upgrades, that is its use, character quartz and character weapons. (Git-FED, 2026-11-22 ~10:31 EST)
- All Arts are jobs for Bondkin, added to the FFTA job list (Git-FED, 2026-11-22 ~12:52 EST). Skills are learned via items, and skills attach to weapons like FFTA: equip the weapon, learn the skill. The 51 CS3 Art names in entry 52 are the skill list for those Bondkin Arts jobs. Quartz, Shards, and Core Shards stay character only. Quartz / Lattice items and Arts-related items are listed in section 6. This supersedes the old rule that Arts are used less often and mostly passive / healing.
- Every item is graded: F 0, E 1-21, D 22-51, C 52-79, B 80-99, A 100-139, S 140-169, SS 170-199, SSS 200-249, SSSS 250+. A grade score measures the total combat power of a piece of gear, computed from its stat bonuses plus the strength of its effect, set when the gear is made, found, or dropped, and it rises when the gear is enhanced, each upgrade step adds its stat gains to the score. Grade changes what gear does: at grade A the bonus effect gains one step, at grade S it gains a second step, at grade SS and above it unlocks a bonus passive. Higher grade gear teaches higher AP abilities.
- Currency is the Crest, symbol Cr. Wallet cap 999,999 Cr, start 3,000 Cr. Crests are earned as prize money from winning battles, by selling items and materials at shops, from mission and dispatch rewards, and from clan turf payouts. Prices below are only given where a source states one, in that source's own currency (Crests, gil, Silver, Recycled Material, sepith, U-Material). All mira prices from the source games convert to Crests 1:1.
- An item is listed once, in its main category. Cross-references say "see" and are not counted again.
- If a source is unverified, the entry is flagged UNVERIFIED. If a category has no source items, this file says so plainly and does not fill it.
- Do not invent items, do not invent values. Values below are only where a source states them.

## 1. Character Items (class items merged here)

For the trainer only. Class items are not a separate named list: no individual class item is named in the sources. Class weapons and class gear are the character weapons and armor below, used by the character's class. Class flow: Class, Advanced Class, Twin Path, Twin Ascension. Opening choice: Sword, Shield, Staff. The opening choice affects the main player.
Sources: Puffkin shops, mission rewards, trade shop, treasure chests, New Game Plus carry-over. Puffkin shops sell items, weapons, armor, and accessories, and host synthesis. Puffkin shops have 3 tiers: Tier 1 at game start, Tier 2 after clearing 4 disconnected worlds, Tier 3 after clearing 8 disconnected worlds, better stock at each tier. The Trade Shop is separate from a Puffkin shop. It trades in sepith and U-Material, accepts sepith of any element, U-Material, and duplicate gear, and gives Bell Shards, Special Shards, blueprints, and rare crafting materials that Puffkin shops never sell. One Trade Shop sits in the main town of each disconnected world, first found in Saltquay on the Realm Map. Equipment slots: Mankind gets 6 slots, weapon, armor, helmet, gloves, shoes, accessory. A Bondkin gets 5 slots, weapon, armor, helmet, shoes, accessory, no glove slot. Buying a certain weapon can let the holder use a certain skill, under the item to attack rule. Weapon to Art mapping by element: Earth Arts to hammers and heavy blades, Water Arts to rods and staves, Fire Arts to swords and blades, Wind Arts to bows and spears, Time Arts to daggers and scythes, Space Arts to guns and orbs, Mirage Arts to whips and fans. Which specific weapon grants which specific Art skill is not given in the sources.

1. Soulblade: the main player weapon. Equippable and upgradable, with U-Materials. Obtained as the opening choice weapon, it starts unupgraded. Upgrade steps: Customize I, U-Material x2, raises attack and adds 5% critical. Customize II, U-Material x2, raises attack again and adds 10% critical. Customize III, U-Material x3, raises attack again and adds a small elemental bonus matching the current world weapon theme. Ultimate form, 1 Darkstone Ore plus U-Material x5, unlocks the Soulblade's full power and its top skill. Darkstone Ore is found in a late dungeon, not bought. The Soulblade cannot be crafted, it is only upgraded with U-Materials. Customization happens at an Orbal Factory or workshop, not in the field.
2. Sword: character weapon category, opening choice item.
3. Shield: character armor category, opening choice item.
4. Staff: character weapon category, opening choice item.
5. Weapon categories: Blade, Saber, Dagger, Katana, Spear, Axe, Bow, Gun, Fists. No individual named weapon is given in the sources, only categories, except the 12 named world weapons, one per disconnected world: Vexbrand (The Thirteen headquarters), Doorwarden Glaive (The Thirteen's dark corridors), Whisperfang (The Thirteen's secrecy), Spellweave Staff (magic multiverse), Empath Bow (mind and emotions world), Thornrose Rapier (fairy tale kingdom), Geartooth Axe (hidden tech kingdom), Sunmane Spear (lion kingdom savanna), Tidecaller Trident (underwater kingdom), Wavecrest Cutlass (ocean island), Pawprint Hammer (animal city), Gravebell Flail (haunted house). Axe is also a gathering tool, see entry 28.
6. Armor: character armor category. No individual armor piece is named.
7. Elemental weapon: elemental weapons carry elemental weakness and resistance. Elements: Earth, Fire, Wind, Water, Time, Space, Mirage, plus Dark (Hollow variants) and Ghost (Echo variants). Which weapon carries which element, and how much weakness or resistance it gives, is not given in the sources.
8. Elemental armor: elemental armor carries elemental weakness and resistance. Same element note as entry 7.
9. Shoes: character gear category, upgradable with U-Materials. No individual shoe is named.
10. Gloves: character gear category. No individual glove is named.
11. Accessories: character gear category, upgradable with U-Materials. No individual accessory is named, except the Arts-boosting accessories in section 6, entries 64-72.
12. Costumes: collected as gear. No individual costume piece is named in the sources.
13. Crafts: character specific techniques costing CP. CP is built by attacking and taking damage. S-Crafts are super moves, 100+ CP, stronger at 200+ CP. S-Break can be pressed anytime at 100+ CP. CP-restoring consumable items are listed in entry 98. Crafts are techniques, not items, listed here because class items have no separate named list.

## 3. Character Tools and Homestead (synthesis merged here)

Character tools are separate from creature items. No tool in this section is a Bondkin item or a monster item. Synthesis combines collected items to make new items, hosted at Puffkin shops. Recipes are fixed and listed, found as recipe scrolls in treasure chests and boss rewards, plus Free Development for basic items once 5 material types have been collected. Two hard gates: blueprints are required to make a thing in the first place, no blueprint, no craft. A workbench is required to even get to work, no workbench, no crafting. Blueprints come from the Trade Shop. Which workbench each recipe needs is not given in the sources.

27. Blueprint: required to make a thing. No blueprint, no craft. Blueprints come from the Trade Shop, and vehicle blueprints come from the camp points shop. Weapon and armor synthesis recipes also need a blueprint. Vehicle blueprint, generic, see entry 36: required to craft vehicles at Mechanic Station, alongside materials. No specific blueprint item name found, no per-vehicle material recipe found, except the transport build materials in entry 77. Do not invent these.
28. Axe: gathering tool. Character, Logger role. Chop wood.
29. Pickaxe: gathering tool. Character, Miner role. Mine stone and ore.
30. Fishing rod: gathering tool. Character, Angler role. Fish.
31. Workbench: crafting station. Character, homestead. Required to craft: no workbench, no crafting. Crafts weapons, armor, drones, unfinished goods, weapon attachments, equipment modification materials, modified modules, and Repair Parts. Not a vehicle crafter.
32. Construction Workbench: primary workbench. Character, homestead. Crafts all other workbenches and furniture, including the Mechanic Station. Place first at the homestead.
33. Raw Material Workbench: refining station. Character, homestead. Refines gathered raw materials into usable crafting items. Without it raw materials cannot be used for crafting or trading.
34. Deconstruction Bench: dismantling station. Character, homestead. Dismantle unwanted materials to Recycled Material currency. Obtained via Adventure Guidebook / main story.
35. Equipment Repair Table: repair station. Character, homestead. Repairs weapons, armor, drone, gas mask only, not vehicles. Craft at Construction Workbench.
36. Mechanic Station: vehicle station. Character, homestead. Crafts vehicles (bikes and four-wheeled vehicles) and repairs all vehicles / restores durability. Craft at Construction Workbench, furniture tab: 150 Wood + 75 Stone, then place at homestead. Also said to be obtained through story progression. Homestead level gates its build formula.
37. Homestead Sign: homestead upgrade point. Character. Upgrades happen at the Homestead Sign outside the home. Homestead levels gate character level: Lv1 caps character at level 15, Lv2 unlocks to level 25, farmland active at Lv3, relocate available at Lv2.
38. Storage cabinet: homestead storage. Character. The backpack holds a fixed amount, a storage cabinet at the homestead stores extra. How much extra a cabinet holds, and how many cabinets a homestead can have, is not given in the sources.
39. Homestead building parts: Wall, Roof, Stairs, Door, Decorative furnishing. Building uses gathered materials.
40. Farmland: homestead, active at Lv3. Building and farming use gathered materials.
41. Weapon crafting: craftable weapons are basic swords, blades, sabers, bows, staves, and shields, made at the Workbench from Wood, Steel, and ore, every craft needs a blueprint first, no blueprint means no craft. The Soulblade cannot be crafted, it is only upgraded with U-Materials. Unique weapons and world weapons cannot be crafted or modified, only upgraded.
42. Weapon modification system: kept. Modification changes one stat or adds one elemental effect to a crafted weapon, and costs Steel plus Screw plus U-Material for Mankind weapons. Bondkin weapons are not modified with U-Materials, U-Material stays Mankind only.
43. Gear progression: equipment is viewed and enhanced. How enhancement changes a piece, what it costs, and how far it can go, is not given in the sources.
44. Repair Parts: repair weapons, armor, drone, gas mask, NOT vehicles. Craftable at Workbench, buyable in Recycle Shop. Do NOT file as a vehicle part.

## 4. Bondkin / Creature Items

For Bondkin only, the trained partner, cap 200. Categorized by the FFTA item listing. Bondkin learn ALL action, reaction, support, combo, and movement abilities from weapons and accessories. A Bondkin equips only one ability and one skill at a time, one passive and one active, abilities cost AP to equip. Every skill use takes a turn. Which weapon grants which skill, AP costs, and whether a learned skill stays after the gear is removed, are not given in the sources. Bondkin gear categories are the same weapon, armor, shoe, glove, and accessory categories in section 1. They are not counted twice here. Bondkin gear is separate instances from character gear: character gear and Bondkin gear do not cross over. No individual Bondkin weapon, armor piece, shoe, glove, or accessory is named, only categories.

45. Growth Item: to train and grow Bondkin. Found in the Monster Bank, shops, missions. What it does in detail, which stat or level effect, how much, and when it can be used, is not given in the sources.
46. Soul: Morpher item. A Hunter captures a Feral, it goes to The Bank, it drops a Soul, a Morpher-type Bondkin equips the Soul and morphs into that monster type in battle, with power tied to Bank feeding. Morpher forms, 9 types: Goblin, Flan, Bomb, Dragon, Lamia, Bug, Panther, Foulbloom, Floateye. Morph learned per type, 200 AP each in the source system.
47. Satyr Flute: Animist instrument. Learned from Satyr Flute: Chocobo Rush, 300 AP, 12 MP, 45 WATK, Double Line, a chocobo stampede attack, not transport.
48. Beastmaster instruments: Bell, Flute, Harp. Bondkin, Elderkin only in the source system. Control command, learned from instruments. Control types, 13: Goblin, Flan, Bomb, Dragon, Lamia, Bug, Grudgeknife, Panther, Foulbloom, Floateye, Undead, Fairy, Rockbeast. Control is not transform, not capture. It controls an enemy monster's actions for a turn.
49. Totem Spirit: a guardian special attack for a Bondkin bloodline. Each bloodline gets a Totem Spirit. A Totem Spirit attack is a special attack, a move a Bondkin can use while Mega evolved, or as a Z-move if Mega is not used. Totem Spirits are Stage 3 only. The 5 Totem Spirit names: Hybrid Dawnwarden, Puffkin Brightpom Sovereign, Elderkin Quiet Flame Sage, Harefolk Moonhare Matriarch, Drakescale Emberwyrm King.

## 5. Monster Items

For monsters only, wild Feral. Monsters do not have weapons or equipment. They have monster related items only. Growth Item and Soul, see entries 45-46, are shared after catch, not counted again here.

50. Monster Egg: monster breeding egg. Monsters can be bred like animals for eggs. A monster egg is bred, then hatched. No named egg item beyond the generic monster egg is given in the sources for FED-13 monsters.
51. Daycare, not Monster Bank, for Bondkin: Bondkin do NOT have a Monster Bank. They have a daycare place in the game. Monsters are in the Monster Bank. Roster is 24 in The Bank, deploy 6. Creatures are different from monsters in breeding: creatures do not lay eggs, they have full children, that can have a high rarity, but it is triple the time it takes a monster egg to be bred than hatched. Daycare rules: deposit is free, withdrawal costs 100 Cr plus 100 Cr per level gained. A Bondkin gains 1 EXP per step the player walks, does not change sub race or clan in daycare, and learns new moves automatically on leveling, forgetting its oldest move first if it already knows four. A Bondkin left in daycare is fully healed on withdrawal. Beast eggs are checked every 256 steps, and a Bondkin child takes triple the steps of a Beast egg to arrive. No daycare item is named in the sources. Link trading is a later feature, to be created later. No item for it is given in the sources.

## 6. Quartz / Lattice Items (Character Only) and Arts (Bondkin Jobs)

The Lattice is for the CHARACTER, not Bondkin. Quartz, Shards, and Core Shards are character items. Arts are jobs for Bondkin, added to the FFTA job list (Git-FED, 2026-11-22 ~12:52 EST). A Bondkin learns Arts skills via items attached to weapons, like FFTA: equip the weapon, learn the skill. The 51 CS3 Art names in entry 52 are the skill list for those Bondkin Arts jobs. In the source system, Arts split into Attack, Recovery, and Support. CS3 regular Arts are 25 attack vs 26 recovery/support, near 50/50. Pure recovery is only 11 of 51 in CS3.
FLAGGED, UNRESOLVED: entries 64-72 are Arts-boosting accessories listed as character gear in the source system. Arts are now Bondkin jobs, so which side equips an Arts-boosting accessory is Git-FED's call. Do not assume.
The Lattice slots, FED-13 rule, follows ARCUS II (CS3): 1 Main Core Shard slot, 1 Sub Core Shard slot, 7 Shard slots. Main: 100% EXP, full stats, all special abilities. Sub: 50% EXP, first ability only, reduced stats. EP versus MP: Mankind has three bars, HP, EP (blue bar, spent on Arts, restored by EP Charge items and charging stations), and CP (yellow bar, built by attacking and taking damage, spent on Crafts). Bondkin keep their own HP and MP bars for Surge Forms and Bondkin skills. EP and MP are not the same bar, and EP Charge never restores MP or CP.

## 8. Consumables / Healing / Food / Water

Food and water are an extra feature only, not a focus. Going unprepared into the desert should be a problem, but not a big problem. Hunger and hydration each run 0 to 100. A snack restores 25 hunger, a meal restores 50 hunger, a feast dish restores 100 hunger. A sip of water restores 25 hydration, a flask restores 50 hydration, a full waterskin restores 100 hydration.

95. Food: buy food at shops. Cooking is done by the Chef role at a homestead kitchen or a campfire, using gathered ingredients. Six dishes. Trail Stew, meat plus root vegetables, restores 50 HP to the whole party out of battle. Ember Skewers, meat plus fire spice, restores 25 HP and raises attack for the next battle. Tide Soup, fish plus salt, restores 50 MP to one Bondkin. Field Rations, grain plus dried meat, restores 25 hunger and 25 hydration. Sweet Rolls, grain plus honey, restores 25 HP and raises mood in towns. Grand Feast, rare meat plus rare spice plus grain, restores all HP and MP to the whole party and gives double EXP for the next 3 battles. One dish eaten at a time, effects last until the next dish or rest.
96. Water: people give you water, find water, water fountains and vending machines sit where they belong. Water fountains sit in every town square, at every guild hall, and at the homestead. Vending machines sit in cities, at train and ferry stops, in guild halls, and at Battle Frontier entrances, never in dungeons or wild routes. Water fountain and vending machine are places where water is found, not carried items.
97. Drive orb: fills the Surge Gauge, along with taking damage and attacking. The Surge Gauge resource is called Aether. Drive orbs are field only, never sold in shops. A small drive orb restores one tenth of one Surge bar, a large drive orb restores one quarter of one Surge bar, picked up automatically on contact, no inventory use, no battle menu action, two grades only. Drive Recovery, a crafted item, restores one full Surge bar and is usable only from the menu, never in battle. Transforming fully refills HP and MP.
98. Healing items, full set, usable in battle and out of battle. Tonic restores 20 HP, 200 Cr. Strong Tonic restores 50 HP, 700 Cr. Grand Tonic restores 200 HP, 1,500 Cr. Full Tonic restores all HP, 2,500 Cr. Ether restores all MP, 1,200 Cr. Elixir restores all HP and MP, 3,000 Cr. Revive restores a downed Bondkin at half HP, 1,500 Cr. Grand Revive restores a downed Bondkin at full HP, 3,000 Cr. One cure per status, 200 Cr each: Antidote for poison, Burn Salve for burn, Thaw Salve for freeze, Wake Salve for sleep, Calm Salve for paralysis. Full Cure heals all status problems on one Bondkin, 600 Cr. Full Restore restores all HP and all status problems, 3,000 Cr. Healing by spell, by transforming, and by charging station still works alongside items. CP items, usable in battle and out of battle: Tomato Sandwich restores 1500 HP and 10 CP, 500 Cr. ## 9. Mission / Key Items

Story missions run in order, and mission items gate that order. Quests are mandatory content, and quest rewards come with them. Dispatch and guild missions are the reason the 24 clan slots exist.

99. Mission item: story missions in order to get items to go on missions. No single mission item is named by its own name in the sources, beyond the type itself.
100. Dispatch report: return item from a dispatch. A dispatch deploys for 5, 10, or 20 days. The player walks around for a few days till the dispatched party comes back. On return, the dispatch brings back mission items and a dispatch report. How dispatch days are counted is not given in the sources, because calendar days passing as the player moves on the map was scrapped.
101. Quest reward: category, not a single item. Every quest carries a quest reward. No reward table per quest is given in the sources.
102. Judge points, Yellow card, Red card, Jail, Law card, Anti law: tournament items and score, one entry. Judge and laws apply at tournaments only, not everywhere. Jail means contest jail only, for the duration of the match or tournament, not real jail.
103. Treasure chest: dungeon chest, holds items from the named item pool. Whether chest contents differ by dungeon, or whether any chest holds a fixed item, is not given in the sources.
104. Prison / Card share: kept side systems. What items, if any, prison or card share uses, is not given in the sources.

Note on count: entries 99-104 are 6 entries, bringing the numbered total to 104, not 98, because entries 39-44, 52-72, and 79-94 are family entries that each contain several named items. The lean main list is 104 numbered entries, 98 of them are core playable items, 6 are category / system entries with no named item (entries 13, 99, 101, 103, 104, and the class note in section 1). That is the honest count, not a padded 450.
