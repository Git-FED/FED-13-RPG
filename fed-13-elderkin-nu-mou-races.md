# FED-13 RPG - Elderkin / Nu Mou Races

Elderkin is the FED-13 name for Nu Mou. Nu Mou is a Final Fantasy name, renamed to Elderkin.

## Decided: 4 Elderkin types

Git-FED decided, 2026-11-22. 4 distinct Elderkin types, divided by game / world, not by biology. Same core race, different look, lore, and culture per world.

1. 🌙 Dream Elderkin - Dream-World Nu Mou, Final Fantasy Tactics Advance
2. 📜 Original Elderkin - Original / Historical Nu Mou, Final Fantasy XII and Revenant Wings
3. ✨ Spirit Elderkin - Spirit / Fae Nu Mou, Final Fantasy XIV: Shadowbringers
4. 📖 Jylland Elderkin - Jylland / Grimoire Nu Mou, Final Fantasy Tactics A2: Grimoire of the Rift

## Core traits, all 4 types

- Dog-like, squat, elongated faces and tails
- Long, thick, droopy ears with a natural hole near the tip, pierced with gold rings, beads, or magical talismans
- White head hair, sandy-orange fur on tails, fur color varies pale cream, brown, reddish-brown
- Skin tone varies by individual, pale, dull, grayish-tan, brown, blue / lavender for Ezel Berbier, darker brown / tan for Babus Swain
- Lifespan more than 3 times Humes, roughly 200 to 250 years
- Look aged from a very young age, wrinkled, white hair, hunched posture, young and old look similar
- Most skilled race in magic, most magically attuned race in Ivalice
- Shun water, avoid it at all cost
- Can control monsters with Beastmaster class
- Totema in FFTA is Ultima, renamed in FED-13, do not reuse Ultima as a Totem name

## 1. Dream Elderkin 🌙

Source: Final Fantasy Tactics Advance, 2003, Ryoma Ito, original baseline, Gran Grimoire dream Ivalice.

- Look: short, compact chibi, 2.5 to 3 heads tall, smooth rounded faces, short muzzle, expressive anime eyes, oversized flat ears that drag near the ground, stubby legs, bright job hats and robes
- Lore: mortal Ivalice mages and monster tamers, storybook / dream world
- Jobs: Black Mage, White Mage, Time Mage, Illusionist, Alchemist, Beastmaster, Sage, Morpher, exclusive to FFTA

## 2. Original Elderkin 📜

Source: Final Fantasy XII, 2006, and Revenant Wings, 2007, Akihiko Yoshida, canonical historical baseline, real Ivalice.

- Look: 4 to 5 heads tall, broad-shouldered, heavy torso, deep hunch, leathery deeply wrinkled skin, long canine snout, sunken eyes, heavy thick textured ears with large gold rings, wild white manes, heavy layered ceremonial robes
- Lore: ancient foundational sentient race of Ivalice, reserved scholarly historians and Kiltia sect acolytes, Mt. Bur-Omisace, preserving legends of the Espers, tending pilgrims and war wounded, oral tradition and ancestral memory
- Names: two-part apostrophe names, Ma'kenroh, Ma'Kleou, Roh'Kenmou, Roh'Kenmu, Ashkroft
- Clans / orders: Kiltia Sect, Kenmou / Kleou Elder Line, sages guarding ancient secrets

## 3. Spirit Elderkin ✨

Source: Final Fantasy XIV: Shadowbringers, 2019, The First, Il Mheg, Fae redesign.

- Look: 3.5 heads tall, shrunken FFXII rig, softer plush silhouette, rounded snout, softer skin, brighter glowing eyes, shorter wider floppy ears, moss / nature accented hair, nature woven Il Mheg fae attire, leaves, bark, moss
- Lore: members of the Fae, not a standard mortal race, loyal servile caretakers, crafters, soul-magic researchers, Beq Lugg mastered ancient soul-weaving magic, a taboo skill, accompanied by familiars called porxies, crafted from clay and water and brought to life with magic
- Names: two-word Elder Futhark rune names, Beq Lugg, Sul Uin, Sul Oul, Daen Kenn, Gyuf Daen, Ys Gyuf, Eo Sigun, Lugg Aenc, Wyd Aenc, Wyd Lad, Marn Ose, Nee Marn, Tyr Nee
- Factions: Artisans of Pla Enni, traditionalist fae, mushroom houses, clay familiars, ancient honor code of equal exchange, and the Lawless Ones, outcasts who abandoned the code, Father Collard's Chambers

## 4. Jylland Elderkin 📖

Source: Final Fantasy Tactics A2: Grimoire of the Rift, 2007, Nintendo DS sprite refinement, Jylland region of Ivalice.

- Look: reverted to FFTA compact storybook proportions, sharper DS pixel art, more detailed shading, expressive dialogue portraits, new job outfits for Scholar and Arcanist, social faction coding for named leaders
- Lore: Jylland clans, mercenary guilds competing for territory, quests, auctions, aristocratic Nu Mou Nobles led by Madreth, criminal syndicate Duelhorn co-led by Duke Snakeheart, Zedlei Consortium and Scholars' Guilds
- Jobs: Black Mage, White Mage, Time Mage, Illusionist, Alchemist, Beastmaster, Sage, Scholar, exclusive to FFTA2, Arcanist, exclusive to FFTA2, Transmuter, exclusive to FFTA2, brought back 2026-11-22, Morpher dropped
- Transmuter, 10th Nu Mou job, mix items on the battlefield, brought back 2026-11-22, sprite and portrait still exist, reused for Luck-stick sellers and NPCs in Brightmoon Tor

## Elderkin jobs, all types

FFTA and FFTA2, 8 base jobs, plus special jobs:
- Black Mage, offensive elemental magic
- White Mage, healing and restorative magic
- Time Mage, haste, slow, gravity
- Illusionist, wide-area magic, all enemies
- Alchemist, support and debuff, transmuting items, status ailments, only Nu Mou job that can use the item command without it being equipped in FFTA
- Beastmaster, controls monsters on the field to use their abilities
- Sage, heavy offensive and supportive mage, high stat growth
- Morpher, FFTA only, transforms into captured monsters
- Scholar, FFTA2 only, tome-based magic, affects all units
- Arcanist, FFTA2 only, dark / arcane magic, targets specific unit levels or statuses
- Transmuter, FFTA2 only, mix items on the battlefield, brought back 2026-11-22

Special unique Elderkin jobs, evolutions in FED-13, not base jobs:
- Hermetic, Ezel Berbier, creator of Anti-Laws, battlefield manipulation, status effects, rule-breaking, Astra, Silence, Sleep
- Runeseeker, Babus Swain, Prince Mewt's personal attendant, mace-wielding magic, Sage-level stat growth, Quarter cuts target HP by 75%, Overpower, Explode

## How they look different, and why

- In universe: magic and aging, heavy exposure to Mist, arcane energies, transmutation causes pigmentation shifts, Ezel blue-violet skin, others pale, sandy, dark brown. Long life, 3x Humes, wrinkles and hunched posture early.
- Visual readability: dozens of units share a small isometric grid, artists gave each job and named boss a distinct silhouette, color palette, hat / robe style.
- Different art directors and world tones: Ryoma Ito FFTA / FFTA2 storybook, Akihiko Yoshida FFXII grounded political epic, FFXIV Shadowbringers fairy-tale First.
- Clans do NOT change biology: Nu Mou in rival clans use the same generic job sprites, differentiated by palette swaps, job attire, and leader portraits only. Clothing and accessories show clan / faction, not body type.
- Generations: no visual generational stages, no young vs elder visual type. Multiple generations live alongside one another for centuries. Closest named generations are the Kenmou / Kleou clan of elder sages in FFXII.

## FED-13 use

- Elderkin is one of the 5 Bondkin bloodlines, #003 in the Codex, after Hybrid #001 and Puffkin #002.
- Elderkin jobs are the Elderkin Craft pool, see new-game-crafts-per-race.md and new-game-crafts-ffta-races.md.
- Elderkin sub races / clans in FED-13: Cairnwick, The Quiet Flame Order, Mount Veylspire, worker proposals, not individually approved by Git-FED yet.
- Elderkin young title: Apprentice / Child / Young Scholar, do NOT use Pup / Kit. Elder title: Elder / Master Scholar / High Elder / Master Acolyte.
- The 4 types above are design / lore variants, not 4 separate bloodlines. Core stays 5.
