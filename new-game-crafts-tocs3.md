## 0. Gauge clarification — CP, IV, BP, OB — READ THIS FIRST

This is the point the task asked me to clarify, and the sources conflict in wording:

- Crafts drain CP — Craft Points. Every English guide checked (GameFAQs, RPGFan, Gematsu, NIS America trailer, Neoseeker) says Crafts consume CP, S-Crafts need 100+ CP, max CP is 200, S-Crafts consume all CP, and are stronger at 200 CP. CP is built by attacking and taking damage, and lost entirely if the character is K.O.'d. S-Break = using an S-Craft on any turn, even the enemy's, at 100+ CP.
- The live Kiseki Wiki page I read displays the same costs as "OB", not "CP" — e.g. Crimson Slash "20 OB", Gale "80 OB", S-Crafts "100+ OB". The search-index cache of the same page shows "20 CP", "80 CP", "100+ CP". The page's own units note still says "# HP/# EP/# CP". I have not found what OB stands for, and I did not treat it as a separate gauge. In the tables below I use CP, the English in-game term, and flag the OB/CP display discrepancy as UNVERIFIED.
- IV is NOT a separate Craft gauge in CS3 in any source I checked. The earlier Arts worker reported "IV Regen" for Impassion etc. In the Kiseki Arts list I checked, Impassion is "Restores 30 CP (4 turns)" / "CP Regen (30 CP/t, 4t)", Heat Up is "Restores 20 CP". "IV" appears to be a mistranslation / alternate rendering of CP in that Arts source. Do not treat IV as a third gauge. If a later source uses IV, it means CP.
- Brave Orders drain BP — Brave Points, NOT CP. BP max is 5, carries between battles. BP is gained by Link Attacks / Assists (1 BP per Unbalance follow-up), critical hits, etc. BP is also spent on Link Rush (3 BP) and Link Burst (5 BP). Brave Orders cost 1-5 BP, do not cost a turn, only one Order can be active at a time, activating a new one cancels the old, and they can be cancelled free.
- Panzer Soldat / Valimar S-Crafts drain BP, not CP. Finisher = 2 BP, Unite Finisher = 5 BP. Their regular Crafts still cost CP.
- Two Crafts drain HP instead of / in addition to CP: Gaius — Golden Howl, 30% Max HP, restores 60 CP; Agate — Wild Rage, 30% Max HP, restores 70 CP. Several Crafts restore CP: Altina Argem Heal +20 CP, Alisa Heavenly Gift +20 CP/turn (3t), Emma Serene Blessing +40 CP, Ash Unbound Rage +50 CP/turn (2t), Tita Vital Cannon +30 CP.

Types: Attack = damage, may have extra effects. Recovery = restores HP/status of allies. Support = buffs allies / debuffs enemies. Delay is AT ticks until the user's next turn. Power / Break are internal % values, for relative comparison only. Unbal. = Unbalance chance bonus, or Yes = can Unbalance.

Counts, from the Kiseki page as read:
- Playable characters with on-foot Crafts in CS3: 25 (Rean, Juna, Kurt, Altina, Musse, Ash, Alisa, Elliot, Laura, Machias, Emma, Jusis, Fie, Gaius, Millium, Sara, Claire, Agate, Tio, Sharon, Olivier, Angelica, Aurelia, Tita, Lechter) + Valimar as a separate pilot section.
- On-foot entries: 124 total — 96 regular Crafts + 28 S-Crafts. Tio and Tita have no S-Craft listed on the CS3 page — flagged below, not invented.
- Panzer Soldat variants: 18 more entries (8 regular Crafts + 10 BP Finishers/Unite Finishers) for Juna, Kurt, Musse, Ash, plus Valimar's 6 already counted above.
- All Crafts are character-specific only. No Craft in CS3 is shared between two on-foot characters, except the Panzer Soldat versions which reuse the pilot's Craft names with different costs/effects, and Rean/Valimar sharing Crimson Slash / Crescent Flash / Helix names.

## 28. What is character-specific, and what is NOT a Craft
- Every regular Craft and S-Craft above is character-specific only — no two on-foot characters share a Craft name, except Rean/Valimar sharing Crimson Slash / Crescent Flash / Helix, and the Panzer versions reusing the pilot's names.
- Detector / Analyze family is NOT one shared Craft: Altina Detector-Delta, Elliot Detector-alpha, Emma Detector-beta, Tio Analyze are four separate character-specific Crafts with different debuffs (STR/SPD, STR/ATS, DEF/ADF, STR/DEF respectively).
- Brave Orders are also character-specific — one per character, except Rean (4). They are not Crafts: they cost BP, not CP, and do not cost a turn.
- EX Arts are not Crafts: the mech partner EX Arts (Geo Quake, Aqua Matter, etc.) cost EP, not CP/BP, and belong to the Arts system.
